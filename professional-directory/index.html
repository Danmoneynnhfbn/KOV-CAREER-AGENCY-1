PROFESSIONAL DIRECTORY / PRIVACY PRINCIPLES

Professional discovery, with consent at its core.

KOV is not publishing personal profiles on this page. These standards explain how any future directory listings must be scoped: with a clear purpose, voluntary participation, and accountable data handling.

Defined purpose

Profile format

Any listing should help professionals understand relevant experience and expertise, with clear context about its scope.

Participation

Professionals should choose whether to participate, what appears publicly, and when to update or withdraw their profile.

Privacy by design

Data lifecycle

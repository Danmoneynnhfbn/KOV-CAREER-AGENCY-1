# KOV master commercial service register

**Prepared:** 27 November 2026  
**Purpose:** Establish one accurate commercial source of truth for the public KOV Career Agency website.

## Commercial principle

Lead with the professional's situation and the outcome they need. Explain the difficulty they are facing, what a useful improvement would look like, how KOV can help, what the work includes, what it does not include, and how to begin an enquiry. Technology, internal process labels and SEO terminology are not the sales proposition.

KOV is a candidate-side career representation agency and provider of separate individual career services. It is not an employer-side recruitment agency, job board, mass-application subscription or self-service SaaS product.

## Primary managed service

### 1. Private Career Representation

- **Customer:** Suitable senior and experienced professionals who want help coordinating a focused career search, including discreet or international moves.
- **Customer situation:** The professional is busy, the search lacks direction, relevant employers or decision-makers are difficult to identify, outreach is inconsistent, or the work of research and follow-up is hard to manage alongside current responsibilities.
- **Desired benefit:** A more focused and coordinated search, a clearer target market, credible positioning, considered access to relevant professional contacts, and disciplined follow-through.
- **KOV work:** Assess fit and objectives; agree target roles, organizations and markets; research relevant companies and potential contact routes; clarify positioning; prepare and coordinate approved outreach; follow up and review responses; support interview and offer preparation where included in the agreement.
- **Possible deliverables:** Agreed search strategy; target-organization research; decision-maker/contact research where appropriate; positioning recommendations; approved outreach materials and activity; progress and response reviews; agreed interview or offer-preparation support.
- **Boundaries:** Scope, cadence, approvals, responsibilities, communication and fees are agreed in writing. Employers control hiring decisions. KOV does not promise interviews, introductions, offers, employment or timelines. Resume, LinkedIn and other substantial document-development work is separately scoped unless expressly included.
- **Canonical page:** `services.html`; supporting process page `how-it-works.html`.
- **Related pages:** `reverse-recruiting.html`, `services/reverse-recruiting.html`, `services/executive-career-representation.html`, `services/executive-job-search-support.html`, and role/industry/market subject pages.
- **Status:** Existing core service. Keep it the primary managed offering.

## Individual Career Services

These are separate, inquiry-led services. A customer can request one without purchasing managed representation. Each page must make its own customer problem, work product and boundaries clear.

### 2. Resume and CV Writing & Optimization

- **Customer:** Professionals whose CV/resume is unclear, unfocused, difficult to scan, too task-based, or not aligned with the roles they intend to pursue.
- **Desired benefit:** A truthful, readable document that makes relevant scope, contribution, skills and evidence easier to understand.
- **KOV work:** Review the source document and target direction; verify career facts with the client; improve structure, language, evidence and relevance; prepare agreed editable and final formats.
- **Deliverables:** Agreed resume/CV draft(s), document structure and formatting, and final Word/PDF files as agreed.
- **Boundaries:** No invented achievements, metrics, qualifications or responsibilities. Tailored versions, extensive research, translations and additional documents are included only if agreed.
- **Canonical page:** `resume-cv-writing.html`.
- **Status:** Existing standalone service.

### 3. LinkedIn Profile Optimization

- **Customer:** Professionals whose LinkedIn profile does not communicate their real expertise, career direction, leadership scope or professional story consistently.
- **Desired benefit:** A coherent, accurate public profile that supports how the professional wants to be understood.
- **KOV work:** Review profile purpose and target audience; align headline, About, Experience, skills and selected sections; prepare approved profile copy and visual recommendations where included.
- **Deliverables:** Agreed profile content and implementation guidance; profile banner/visual direction only when scoped.
- **Boundaries:** No promise of ranking, reach, recruiter views, leads or job offers. Account operation, posting, paid promotion and ongoing social-media management are not assumed.
- **Canonical page:** `linkedin-profile-optimization.html`.
- **Status:** Existing standalone service.

### 4. Career Documents

- **Customer:** Professionals who need a specific document for a professional decision, application, leadership opportunity or stakeholder conversation.
- **Desired benefit:** The right document communicates the right evidence and context for its intended reader.
- **KOV work:** Identify the document's purpose and audience; gather and verify source information; draft and refine the agreed document.
- **Deliverables:** Depending on the brief, a cover letter, professional biography, leadership summary, executive biography, leadership portfolio narrative, role-specific summary or outreach document.
- **Boundaries:** Only the named documents and revision scope are included. Legal, immigration, financial or regulated advice is excluded.
- **Canonical page:** `career-documents.html`.
- **Status:** Existing standalone service.

### 5. Career Portfolio Development

- **Customer:** Professionals whose value is demonstrated through projects, case studies, publications, products, transformation work, creative output or other evidence that a CV alone cannot show well.
- **Desired benefit:** Selected evidence is organized into a portfolio that helps a reader understand the professional's contribution, method and results.
- **KOV work:** Select suitable evidence; establish confidentiality and permission boundaries; structure case studies and supporting material; organize the agreed portfolio format.
- **Deliverables:** Agreed portfolio structure and content, selected case studies/work samples, and final digital files or web-ready content as scoped.
- **Boundaries:** Client must have permission to share supplied work. Confidential employer/client information must be removed or authorized. Web development is not assumed unless agreed.
- **Canonical page:** `career-portfolio-development.html`.
- **Status:** Existing standalone service.

### 6. Personal Career Website

- **Customer:** Professionals who need an owned, shareable professional destination beyond a CV or third-party profile.
- **Desired benefit:** A clear professional home for biography, expertise, experience, selected work and contact information.
- **KOV work:** Define audience and purpose; plan information architecture; prepare agreed copy and visual direction; build or coordinate the agreed site; review responsive presentation and handover.
- **Deliverables:** Scope-dependent website pages, content, visual implementation and handover notes.
- **Boundaries:** Domain, hosting, paid services, maintenance, integrations, analytics, ongoing updates and third-party licences are separate unless listed in the written quotation. Do not promise search ranking or inbound leads.
- **Canonical page:** `personal-career-website.html`.
- **Status:** Existing standalone service.

### 7. Career Positioning & Optimization

- **Customer:** Professionals considering a promotion, transition, new function, sector move, international market or a change in level who are unsure how their experience maps to the next opportunity.
- **Desired benefit:** A realistic, evidence-based direction and a clearer explanation of fit, transferable experience, gaps and next steps.
- **KOV work:** Clarify the decision and constraints; review career evidence; compare target directions and role requirements; identify positioning strengths and gaps; produce agreed recommendations.
- **Deliverables:** Positioning assessment, target-role criteria, evidence/gap review, professional narrative or action plan as agreed.
- **Boundaries:** No guarantee that a target role is available or that an employer will accept transferable experience. Resume/profile writing, ongoing coaching and managed outreach are separate unless included.
- **Canonical page:** `career-positioning-optimization.html`.
- **Status:** Existing standalone service. Do not create a duplicate PPI page until KOV confirms a distinct deliverable and commercial offer.

### 8. Career Coaching

- **Customer:** Professionals who need structured thinking or preparation for a decision, transition, interview, leadership conversation or offer discussion.
- **Desired benefit:** A clearer decision framework, stronger preparation and practical next actions.
- **KOV work:** Agree the topic and outcome; understand relevant context; use structured discussion, exercises and practice; identify actions and review progress where multiple sessions are agreed.
- **Deliverables:** Agreed coaching session(s), preparation/practice and any written notes or exercises expressly included.
- **Boundaries:** Coaching does not include job applications, employer outreach, document writing, psychotherapy, legal/financial advice or guaranteed outcomes unless a separate scope expressly covers a relevant service.
- **Canonical page:** `career-coaching.html`.
- **Status:** Existing standalone service.

## Supporting workstreams that are not automatically separate products

The following activities may be part of a managed representation engagement or separately scoped only when the business confirms a standalone offer. Do not create a new service page solely because a phrase has search volume:

- Target-company and market research
- Decision-maker and recruiter research
- Professional outreach and follow-up
- Interview preparation
- Executive interview coaching
- Offer and negotiation preparation
- Executive resume or LinkedIn strategy
- Career strategy for senior leaders

Existing specialist pages should explain the specific senior-level problem and link to the relevant primary service. They should not imply a separate product, guarantee or deliverable that is not commercially confirmed.

## Naming and relationship rules

- "Reverse recruiting" is a familiar explanatory term for candidate-side search support; it is not a second managed product separate from Private Career Representation.
- "Professional Positioning Intelligence (PPI)" may describe KOV's assessment/positioning thinking, but should not be promoted as a separate paid product until its deliverables and price basis are explicitly established.
- Keep managed Private Career Representation distinct from standalone Individual Career Services.
- A role, industry, country, city or seniority variation is a subject page, not automatically a new service.
- A service page is justified only when the customer problem, KOV work, deliverables and boundaries are genuinely distinct.

## Enquiry routes

- General and service enquiries: `contact.html` and existing service-specific query parameters.
- Managed representation: `services.html` and `how-it-works.html`.
- Individual services: `candidate-services.html` and the seven canonical individual service pages.
- Pricing: `pricing.html`, which must explain scope-first quotation rather than inventing a universal fee.

## Content quality gate

Every service page must answer, in plain language:

1. What situation makes this service relevant?
2. What problem is the customer trying to solve?
3. What useful change should the work support?
4. What does KOV actually do?
5. What will the customer receive?
6. What must the customer provide or approve?
7. What is not included or guaranteed?
8. How is scope and price agreed?
9. What is the next step?
10. Which adjacent service is genuinely relevant, and why?

A page that cannot answer these questions distinctly should be improved or consolidated into the correct existing page, not duplicated.

# KOV expanded service-page candidate catalogue

**Prepared:** 28 November 2026  
**Planning horizon:** 120 service-page candidates across eight customer-problem families.  
**Status:** Internal planning register only. It does not create or confirm 120 separate paid products.

## Why this register exists

The public site currently has one primary managed service and seven standalone Individual Career Services. There are legitimate opportunities to explain specialist needs more clearly, but a search phrase or a different audience label is not automatically a distinct service.

The site also already contains eight published executive-specialist routes under `/services/`. Those are existing URLs, not eight additional paid products. Their mapping to the core service is recorded in `development/KOV-EXISTING-SPECIALIST-SERVICE-ROUTES.csv`; they must be improved or retained as specialist entry points, not duplicated with new slugs.

This register separates:
- Existing canonical offers that already have a public page.
- Components and audience variations that belong under an existing service.
- Candidates that may justify a dedicated landing page after evidence and scope are checked.
- Workstreams within Private Career Representation, rather than additional products.
- Capabilities and platform-specific work that KOV must confirm before advertising.

## Test for a separate landing page

A page candidate moves to publication only when all of the following are true:

1. A real visitor problem is distinct from the parent page.
2. The intended buyer and search intent are sufficiently specific to help the right visitor recognise the situation.
3. KOV can explain a real, repeatable work process and the deliverable the client receives.
4. KOV has the skills, resources and rights needed to deliver that work.
5. Scope, client inputs, revisions, confidentiality and exclusions can be stated accurately.
6. The page can include useful specialist guidance that is not simply a rewritten parent page.
7. Its search intent does not compete unnecessarily with an existing canonical URL.
8. The keyword and search-result research supports a separate URL; no volume, difficulty or ranking claim is assumed without evidence.

If a candidate fails the test, it remains a section, FAQ, supporting example or managed-service workstream. It must not become a thin search landing page.

## Publication safeguards

- The eight existing canonical offers remain the current commercial source of truth.
- No proposed candidate is approved for automatic publication merely by appearing in this register.
- Clinical, medical, academic, technical, creative and external-platform pages require capability or specialist-scope validation.
- PPI is not a separate paid product until its distinct method, report, scope and price basis are confirmed.
- Reverse recruiting remains an explanatory term for Private Career Representation, not a separate product.
- Board, executive, founder, author, director, country and industry variants do not automatically create new products.
- Do not promise employment, interviews, recruiter views, search rankings, ATS acceptance, introductions or hiring outcomes.
- Preserve all 18 metadata datasets and their central JSON-LD catalogue.
- Use the established public-page build, page coverage and sitemap process for every approved new URL.

## Keyword research status

This register is a service taxonomy, not a keyword-tool export. Each candidate still needs:
- Current search-result review for the intended audience and geography.
- Search Console query/landing-page evidence where available.
- Competitor/service-page review for the actual customer problem and deliverable.
- Cannibalisation check against the existing 1,424 canonical URLs.
- A decision to publish, combine, retain as a support section or reject.

The next step is to audit live page bodies and map their specific customer needs and internal service handoffs before choosing which candidates deserve a URL.

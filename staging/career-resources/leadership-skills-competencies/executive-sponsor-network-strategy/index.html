Executive Sponsor Network Strategy

Why sponsors matter

Senior leaders often need more than a broad network. They need a few trusted sponsors or advisers who understand their strengths, decision style, and the kind of role they are aiming for.

Build a sponsor map

  • Identify people who understand your leadership brand and can validate your direction.
  • Clarify what you need from them: perspective, access, references, or a thoughtful challenge.
  • Use relationships that are respectful, reciprocal, and grounded in trust.

Frame the ask carefully

Ask for advice, validation, or insight rather than a favour disguised as access. The strongest sponsor relationships are built on clarity, professionalism, and evidence of action.

Senior Leader Communication and Influence Framework

Core principle

Influence at senior level rarely comes from volume. It comes from clarity, credibility, and the ability to align a narrative with the decisions and risks that matter most to the audience.

Five building blocks

  • Context: Explain the strategic problem, not just your preferred answer.
  • Evidence: Use real data, references, and trade-offs to support the argument.
  • Decision logic: Make the choices and assumptions visible.
  • Stakeholder alignment: Describe who is affected and how commitments are managed.
  • Follow-through: Turn a message into action, accountability, and measurable progress.

Questions for reflection

  1. What is the problem the audience actually needs solved?
  2. What evidence has been shared, and what remains unverified?
  3. Where are the risks, trade-offs, or unresolved tensions?
  4. Who needs to align before a decision can be made?

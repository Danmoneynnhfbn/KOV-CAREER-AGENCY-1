Executive Transition Readiness Scorecard

Purpose

Use this tool to assess whether a change in role, sector, or scope is timed well, evidence-backed, and aligned with the leader's current priorities.

Readiness checklist

  • Positioning: Is your value proposition clear, evidence-based, and differentiated from a generic leadership profile?
  • Timing: Are you moving because the opportunity is right, or because you are reacting to pressure or uncertainty?
  • Market scan: Have you reviewed role types, sectors, and geographies against your desired operating context?
  • Evidence quality: Can you explain your decisions, scale, and accountability using verifiable references?
  • Network: Are your relationships strong enough to surface hidden opportunities and create useful introductions?

Decision questions

  1. What problem am I solving by making a move now?
  2. Which role or scope would actually improve my leverage, learning, and impact?
  3. What evidence can I present without relying on vague claims or unsupported statements?
  4. Which relationships, search channels, and references are realistically available to me?
  5. What would I do if the first opportunity is not the right one?

Recommended output

Before you pursue interviews, produce a one-page summary covering your target role, the decision criteria, the evidence you can substantiate, and the minimum conditions that make a move worth it.

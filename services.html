What KOV
actually does.

Four disciplines, executed weekly by a real human team -- not an AI auto-apply tool, not an offshore queue.

Applications
01

High-Volume Job Applications

  • Humans apply to 50--100 vetted, on-target roles per week (when that pace fits your search)
  • Resume customized per posting for ATS -- no AI-written filler
  • Shared dashboard with job approvals and a dedicated search inbox
  • Applications submitted within an hour of posting when possible
  • Every submission screen-recorded for quality assurance
Outreach
02

Outreach On Every Application

  • Recruiter and hiring-manager outreach for each role we apply to
  • Personalized messages that reference the role and your edge
  • LinkedIn connection requests and follow-up cadence handled for you
  • Track replies and interview invites in one place
Positioning
03

Resume & LinkedIn Rewrite

  • Deep rewrite focused on achievements, not job duties
  • Personal editing sessions to retell your career story
  • ATS-optimized formatting without keyword stuffing
  • LinkedIn headline, About, and Experience aligned to your target
Closing
04

Interview Prep & Negotiation

  • Live mock interviews and tactical loop strategy
  • Behavioral, case, and technical drills as needed
  • Offer structuring on base, bonus, equity, and start date
  • Counter-offer scripts written with you, not for you

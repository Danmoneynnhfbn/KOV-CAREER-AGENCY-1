How representation
is approached.

The work is shaped around agreed goals, professional background, suitable targets, and the level of support that fits the engagement.

Assessment
01

Assessment and Fit

  • Discuss your career objectives, priorities, constraints, and the support you are seeking.
  • Review your professional background and the role or market direction you want to pursue.
  • Assess whether representation is suitable for your circumstances and objectives.
  • Agree the scope, responsibilities, approvals, and communication approach before work begins.
  • No employment, interview, hiring, or timeline outcome is promised.
Strategy
02

Target-Company Strategy and Research

  • Define target roles, organizations, and markets relevant to the agreed direction.
  • Research relevant organizations and opportunities using available information.
  • Identify relevant decision-makers and professional contact routes where appropriate.
  • Prioritize research and next steps according to the agreed scope.
Positioning
03

Professional Positioning

  • Clarify a professional narrative grounded in your verified experience.
  • Identify relevant evidence and themes for the agreed target direction.
  • Coordinate approved positioning with the career materials relevant to the engagement.
  • Any separate document or profile-development work is scoped as an Individual Career Service.
Follow-Up
04

Professional Outreach and Follow-Up

  • Prepare professional outreach appropriate to the agreed target and your background.
  • Obtain approvals for outreach where required by the agreed process.
  • Follow up professionally and coordinate responses and agreed next steps.
  • Direct introductions may be made where appropriate and available; they are not guaranteed.

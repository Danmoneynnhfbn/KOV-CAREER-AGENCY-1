UK Executive Compensation Structure Guide

What to review

Executive compensation is rarely a single number. It typically combines fixed salary, variable pay, benefits, share or bonus arrangements, and role-specific risk or context.

Common components

  • Base salary: The anchor pay element, dependent on role scope, accountability, and market context.
  • Annual bonus: Usually tied to company performance, personal goals, and business context.
  • Long-term incentives: Often use equity, share options, or deferred awards to align executives with longer-term value creation.
  • Benefits: Pension, medical cover, car allowance, or relocation support may form part of total package value.

Questions to ask before accepting

  1. What is the real weighting between fixed and variable components?
  2. How is performance measured, and how much discretion exists?
  3. What is the risk if the role is not delivered as expected?
  4. How much of the package is cash, equity, or deferred value?

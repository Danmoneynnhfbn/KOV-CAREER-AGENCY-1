Executive Network and Influence Plan

What an influence plan should do

For senior leaders, a network strategy is not a contact list. It is a way to create productive conversations with people who understand a target sector, a specific company, or a defined leadership challenge.

Build a narrow contact map

  • Prioritise target firms, workers, and sectors where your experience is most relevant.
  • Map who can provide context, validate your direction, or make an introduction.
  • Segment contacts into advisors, peers, decision-makers, and talent scouts.

Three disciplines to keep

  1. Invest in relevance: every outreach should explain why your background matters for the role or issue.
  2. Be useful first: share insight, ask thoughtful questions, and avoid transactional outreach.
  3. Follow-up with structure: keep notes on who said what, what is next, and what evidence you need to share.

Questions to answer before outreach

Which roles matter most? Who is likely to know them? What do you want them to understand? Which conversations are most likely to lead to understanding rather than a dead end?

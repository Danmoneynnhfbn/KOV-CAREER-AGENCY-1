Non-Executive Director Readiness Assessment

Readiness questions

  • What specific governance experience can you credibly offer?
  • Which strategic, risk, or oversight capability distinguishes you from a general executive candidate?
  • Do you understand the time commitment, fiduciary context, and independence expectations of a board role?

What to prepare before pursuing board work

  1. Map your role history against governance and stewardship themes.
  2. Review your ability to challenge constructively and operate without direct management authority.
  3. Clarify any confidentiality, stakeholder, or regulatory issues that affect a board contribution.

Evidence discipline

Board candidacy is strongest when it is grounded in precise governance evidence, not broad claims or generic seniority. Focus on actual decisions, oversight, risk challenge, and strategic stewardship.

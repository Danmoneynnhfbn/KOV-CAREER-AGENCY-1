UK Global Talent Route: Digital Technology

UK Home Office guidance says the Global Talent visa is available to eligible leaders or potential leaders in digital technology, among other fields. For digital technology, many applicants need an endorsement before applying for the visa; people with a listed eligible prize can apply without an endorsement. This page summarizes public guidance and does not assess anyone's eligibility.

This is a specialist route, not a general executive route

The Home Office identifies both technical backgrounds, such as developers, engineers, and data scientists, and business backgrounds, such as commercial, investment, or digital product expertise in qualifying technology companies. It also names fintech, gaming, cyber security, and artificial intelligence among the digital technology sectors covered by its guidance.

A senior title alone does not establish that a person meets the route requirements. Review the current official criteria and the person's own evidence; an employer job offer is not required for this visa route.

Understand the application stages

  1. Check the route: Confirm that the current digital technology guidance covers the person's field and background, or that a listed eligible prize route applies.
  2. Review endorsement requirements: Applicants who are not applying through an eligible prize generally need an endorsement decision about being a leader or potential leader in the field.
  3. Review the visa application: The visa application is a separate step. Use current Home Office instructions for the correct application circumstances and documents.

The Home Office currently identifies Tech Nation as the digital technology endorsing body. Endorsing-body arrangements and official instructions can change; confirm them directly before acting.

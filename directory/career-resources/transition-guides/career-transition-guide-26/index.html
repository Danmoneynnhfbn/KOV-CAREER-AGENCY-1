Purpose and scope

This staging page provides a structured route placeholder for a career transition guide within the KOV programme. It is designed to support candidate qualification, content definition, and internal review without making a public claim about job demand, salary, employer availability, or market ranking.

  • Clarify the role, audience, and problem the page is meant to answer.
  • Separate general career guidance from a verified employer or market claim.
  • Provide a bounded set of source-backed signals and review questions.
  • Keep the route unpublished while research, approval, and serviceability checks remain open.

Working research framework

  1. Define the target audience, career stage, role type, and geography.
  2. Map the page to a clear user intent and the primary decision the reader is trying to make.
  3. Review public source material, employer evidence, and authority signals before using claims or references.
  4. Distinguish between broad labour-market context, role-specific guidance, and verified employer information.
  5. Keep references clear, dated, and limited to what the page actually supports.

Questions this page should answer

  • What distinguishes the target role or market from adjacent career options?
  • Which evidence types are robust, and which must remain qualified or excluded?

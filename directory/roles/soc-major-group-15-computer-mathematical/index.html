Computer and Mathematical Occupations: U.S. SOC Major Group 15-0000

What this page covers

The 2018 U.S. Standard Occupational Classification (SOC) identifies major group 15-0000 as Computer and Mathematical Occupations. The SOC classifies occupations by work performed; it is not an industry directory, credential, job title standard, or career ranking.

The lists below reproduce the minor and broad group titles recorded under this major group in the 2018 SOC structure workbook. They help locate a classification branch; they do not represent every employer's titles or current hiring requirements.

Using the classification

  1. Start with the actual duties and responsibilities of a role, not title alone.
  2. Use the SOC code as a U.S. statistical classification aid, not as evidence of qualifications, seniority, licensing, or suitability.
  3. Check the relevant national occupational framework when researching work outside the United States.
  4. Use the existing KOV role directory to browse available KOV role pages; their presence does not establish a formal SOC mapping or service availability.

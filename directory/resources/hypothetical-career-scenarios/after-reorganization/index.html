An Executive Reassessing Options After a Reorganization

Situation

Imagine an executive whose responsibilities have shifted after an organizational reorganization. The person is deciding whether to seek clarity about the revised role, explore other internal options, or begin an external search.

Questions to investigate

  • What has formally changed in reporting, decision rights, and role expectations?
  • Which details are confirmed, and which are still under discussion?
  • What contractual, notice, confidentiality, or severance questions require qualified advice?
  • Which external options align with the executive's goals and constraints?

Planning exercise

Document confirmed information, request clarification through appropriate channels, and assess alternatives without treating rumors as fact. Get independent advice before making decisions about legal or contractual rights.

This invented scenario is not an account of a real restructuring, termination, dispute, or career result.

A Public-Sector Executive Considering Private-Sector Work

Situation

Imagine an experienced public-sector leader evaluating a private-sector role in a related field. The person is considering how to explain work shaped by public accountability while learning a different organization's commercial priorities and decision structures.

Questions to investigate

  • Which parts of the target role are similar to the person's verified responsibilities?
  • What commercial, customer, or operating knowledge does the role require?
  • Do current or former public duties create disclosure, confidentiality, conflict, or post-employment restrictions?
  • Which matters require guidance from the relevant ethics office or qualified adviser?

Planning exercise

Describe transferable responsibilities in language the target organization can understand, while preserving confidentiality and avoiding claims about decisions the person did not own. Resolve ethics and compliance questions with the proper authority before proceeding.

This invented scenario does not state any public-sector rule or determine whether a particular move is permitted.

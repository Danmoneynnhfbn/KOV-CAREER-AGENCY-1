A Senior Leader Planning a Return After Time Away

Situation

Imagine a senior professional preparing to re-enter work after a period away for personal reasons. The person is deciding how much context to share, which roles to pursue, and whether their knowledge needs refreshing.

Questions to investigate

  • What role scope and working arrangements are compatible with the person's current priorities?
  • Which skills and professional requirements have changed during the time away?
  • What explanation feels accurate, relevant, and appropriately private?
  • Which recent evidence can demonstrate current capability without implying uninterrupted employment?

Planning exercise

Set a realistic target-role scope, review current role requirements, and prepare a concise truthful account of the transition. The person may choose what personal information to disclose, subject to relevant application requirements and local rules.

This invented scenario makes no promise about employer responses or return-to-work outcomes.

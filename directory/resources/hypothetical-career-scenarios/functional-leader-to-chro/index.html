A People Leader Preparing for Broader Executive Scope

Situation

Imagine a senior people-function leader considering a broader executive role. The candidate has deep functional experience and wants to assess expectations involving enterprise strategy, organizational change, leadership-team influence, and governance.

Questions to investigate

  • What enterprise decisions and shared responsibilities are part of the role?
  • Which verified instances of cross-functional work demonstrate influence beyond the home function?
  • What experience gaps are material to this particular organization's priorities?
  • How will the role's mandate and success criteria be described by the appointing organization?

Planning exercise

Review the actual remit, select verified evidence of enterprise contribution, and distinguish personal accountability from team delivery. Identify development needs rather than assuming a title change proves readiness.

This invented scenario does not define a universal CHRO remit or guarantee advancement.

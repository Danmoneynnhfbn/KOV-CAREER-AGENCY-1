FOR EMPLOYERS — GULF COOPERATION COUNCIL

Plan a leadership search across GCC markets.

Define where the role needs to operate, what success requires and which talent pools are relevant before outreach begins.

KOV can discuss independent executive talent research and sourcing for employers considering the Gulf region. Each brief is scoped around the role and target markets; regional coverage is not a claim of local offices.

Discuss GCC Talent SourcingExplore related KOV support

FRAME THE MANDATE

Clarify where leadership responsibilities sit.

A regional remit can mean different things across organizations. Confirm which countries the leader will cover, where decisions are made, what teams they will lead and how the role interacts with local operations.

Those details help shape a fair success profile and prevent a search from relying on geography or job title as a substitute for role fit.

  • Countries and operating footprint
  • Reporting line and decision authority
  • Functional scope and team responsibilities
  • Experience that is essential versus transferable

MAP RELEVANT TALENT

Research the backgrounds that align with the brief.

KOV can research organizations and senior professionals against the agreed success criteria. Relevant experience may come from different markets or sectors; the client and KOV decide what is transferable for the specific mandate.

If outreach is part of the engagement, KOV and the client agree what can be shared and how candidate interest is communicated. Local regulations and employment requirements should be confirmed by qualified sources.

  • Map target organizations and leadership functions
  • Document role-relevant research
  • Prioritize profiles against agreed evidence
  • Use approved, discreet outreach where in scope

SCOPE WITH CARE

Treat the GCC as multiple markets, not one uniform market.

Country-specific conditions, employer requirements and candidate circumstances differ. Search boundaries should therefore be agreed for each role rather than inferred from a regional label.

KOV works remotely where a search is available and suitable. The client remains responsible for hiring decisions, local employment compliance and candidate assessment.

  • Confirm country and employer scope
  • Agree research sources and outreach approvals
  • Set reporting expectations and decision steps
  • Discuss deliverables and fees directly by message

COMMON QUESTIONS

Before you start a conversation.

Does KOV operate an office in every GCC country?

No such claim is made. KOV can discuss remote sourcing support and confirm availability and scope for the specific countries in the brief.

Can KOV advise on local labor law?

KOV's role is talent research and sourcing support. Employers should consult qualified local advisers on employment law, licensing and regulatory requirements.

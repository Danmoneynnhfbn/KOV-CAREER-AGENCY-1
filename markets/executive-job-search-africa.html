GEOGRAPHY AND MARKETS · KOV CAREER AGENCY

Turn a broad Africa search into a focused market plan.

KOV helps senior professionals identify relevant roles and employers across the specific African markets they are considering.

Countries, sectors, employer types, location and cross-border requirements differ substantially. The search should name the target markets and validate role conditions rather than treating Africa as a single market.

Discuss an Africa SearchExplore related KOV support

DEFINE THE CONTEXT

Start with the role mandate, not the country label.

“Executive opportunity in Africa” is too broad to guide a serious search. A chief operating role in a multi-country business, a country managing-director mandate, a finance leadership role in a regulated institution and a board appointment require different evidence and different target organisations.

Begin by describing the work you want to lead and the conditions under which you could realistically take it on. This gives the market research a clear test instead of turning a list of countries into a list of speculative vacancies.

  • Define the function, level of authority, reporting line and operating remit you want to consider
  • Set sector and organisation boundaries, including ownership model, scale, geography and business stage
  • Identify acceptable countries and cities, travel expectations, relocation preferences and language requirements
  • Record current-employer exclusions, confidentiality needs and any contact restrictions before external research begins

RESEARCH BEFORE ACTION

Compare each market against the same practical questions.

Countries and cities should be assessed individually. A company's presence in a market does not prove that a suitable leadership vacancy exists, and a job title alone does not reveal the authority or conditions attached to it. Keep verified information separate from assumptions that still need checking.

  • Role evidence: identify current, attributable evidence of the remit, such as an employer vacancy, a published mandate or a clearly relevant organisation-level development. Record the date and source.
  • Employer fit: test whether the organisation's sector, scale, ownership, country operations and leadership structure match your experience.
  • Practical access: establish whether the role is locally based, regional, remote or relocation-dependent, and note any language, work-authorisation or professional-registration questions that need independent confirmation.
  • Employment conditions: identify which parts of the package require clarification, such as currency, contract type, incentives, benefits, travel or relocation arrangements. KOV does not provide tax, legal or compensation advice.
  • Search route: distinguish a public vacancy from a recruiter-led process, a direct employer conversation or a longer-term relationship-building opportunity. Do not present an unadvertised possibility as an open role.

Useful source checks include employer career pages, attributable recruiter mandates, relevant industry and professional associations, and official government or regulator information for questions about local requirements. A market note should show what the evidence supports, what remains uncertain and what should be verified before action.

MAKE THE COMPARISON ACTIONABLE

Turn the country list into a decision-ready shortlist.

For each market that remains under consideration, a useful shortlist should state why it is relevant to your experience, which organisations or role families merit further review, what evidence supports the interest and what practical questions remain open.

  • Market rationale: why this country or city belongs in the search, based on your mandate and the evidence available
  • Target organisations: a bounded list of employers or organisation types that meet the criteria you agreed
  • Role hypotheses: the leadership responsibilities and level that appear relevant, clearly distinguished from confirmed vacancies
  • Contact map: relevant public professional contact routes or decision-maker roles, where they can be identified responsibly
  • Next action: what to verify, what needs your approval and whether the market should remain in scope

Within an agreed Private Career Representation engagement, KOV can coordinate target research, professional positioning, approved outreach and follow-up. You review the direction and approve external communication. The exact deliverables and activity limits are set out in writing before work begins.

  • Confirm the target and the work included
  • Agree information-sharing and approval preferences
  • Set practical updates and review points

COMMON QUESTIONS

Before you start a conversation.

What should I share to start?

KOV helps senior professionals identify relevant roles and employers across the specific African markets they are considering. Start with the role or question, relevant markets and practical constraints; share sensitive details only when they are needed.

Does this service guarantee a job, hire or timeline?

No. KOV can carry out only the activities agreed in writing. Employers control hiring decisions, and market conditions and timing remain outside KOV’s control.

How are fees handled?

Fees are discussed directly by message, alongside scope and deliverables, and agreed in writing before work begins.

DEFINE THE CONTEXT

Start with the role, market or question—not assumptions.

Countries, sectors, employer types, location and cross-border requirements differ substantially. The search should name the target markets and validate role conditions rather than treating Africa as a single market.

KOV helps senior professionals identify relevant roles and employers across the specific African markets they are considering. The first step is to make the decision criteria clear enough to guide useful research and practical next actions.

  • Prioritize countries and cities
  • Set sector and employer criteria
  • Clarify relocation and work-authorization questions

RESEARCH BEFORE ACTION

Use relevant evidence to set priorities.

Prioritize countries and cities; Set sector and employer criteria; Clarify relocation and work-authorization questions; Coordinate approved outreach and follow-up. These considerations help the professional distinguish a genuine fit from a surface-level match.

Titles and broad market labels do not establish fit by themselves. Confirm the remit, requirements and practical boundaries before any application, outreach or shortlist activity.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Before you start a conversation.

What should I share to start?

KOV helps senior professionals identify relevant roles and employers across the specific African markets they are considering. Start with the role or question, relevant markets and practical constraints; share sensitive details only when they are needed.

Does this service guarantee a job, hire or timeline?

No. KOV can carry out only the activities agreed in writing. Employers control hiring decisions, and market conditions and timing remain outside KOV's control.

How are fees handled?

Fees are discussed directly by message, alongside scope and deliverables, and agreed in writing before work begins.

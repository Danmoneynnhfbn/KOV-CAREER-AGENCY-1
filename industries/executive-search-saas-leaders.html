FOR EMPLOYERS — SAAS AND SOFTWARE

Source SaaS leaders for the company you are building.

Define the stage, product context and leadership outcomes before researching executives across the market.

KOV provides independent global talent sourcing for senior roles where the brief and research scope are a fit. A SaaS label alone does not define the experience an organization needs.

Discuss SaaS Talent SourcingExplore related KOV support

DEFINE THE SAAS CONTEXT

Specify the operating environment behind the role.

SaaS organizations differ in product, customer, business model, company stage, ownership and operating complexity. Those details help distinguish relevant leadership experience from broad software-sector exposure.

The hiring team should agree the mandate, reporting line, resources, growth or transformation priorities and the evidence that will demonstrate success.

  • Company stage and ownership context
  • Product, customer and go-to-market model
  • Leadership remit and team scope
  • Outcomes expected in the role

RESEARCH RELEVANT LEADERS

Look for transferable experience with clear reasons.

Potential talent pools may include leaders from organizations with comparable product, customer, operational or scaling challenges. KOV researches against the brief and records why a profile may be worth review.

Research does not confirm availability, interest or fit. Those questions are established through appropriate conversation and the employer's assessment process.

  • Map target organizations and adjacent markets
  • Assess experience against role criteria
  • Document evidence and open questions
  • Use approved outreach when in scope

KEEP THE PROCESS HONEST

Separate sourcing activity from hiring outcomes.

KOV can support research, prioritization and outreach according to the written engagement. Your hiring team remains responsible for interview design, candidate evaluation, compliance and the final decision.

Before the search starts, the parties agree deliverables, communication and fees directly by message and in writing. No shortlist size, candidate response or hire is promised.

  • Set clear scope and review points
  • Agree what candidate information may be shared
  • Keep hiring decisions with the organization
  • Confirm applicable legal and data requirements

COMMON QUESTIONS

Before you start a conversation.

Which SaaS functions can KOV source?

Possible roles depend on the brief and confirmed availability. The client should specify the leadership function and experience required rather than assume every SaaS role is in scope.

Does KOV guarantee SaaS candidates will be interested?

No. Research can identify potentially relevant profiles, but interest and availability can only be established through appropriate candidate communication.

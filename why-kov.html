WHY KOV

Two Practices. One Founder. A Different Search Model.

Most career services work with professionals. Most search firms work with companies. KOV operates both practices.

For senior professionals, KOV provides private career representation and reverse recruiting. For companies, KOV provides independent global talent sourcing and passive executive research.

The two practices are distinct. The operating philosophy is the same:

Research deeply. Communicate directly. Execute carefully. Protect discretion.

WHAT CONNECTS BOTH SIDES

Search intelligence.

Although the engagements are different, both sides depend on the same basic discipline:

  • Know the market.
  • Know the target.
  • Know where the relevant people are.
  • Know how to reach them.
  • Communicate with context.
  • Follow through and measure the process.

OPEN FEES

Clear scope before work begins.

KOV uses an open-fee model. Candidate engagements do not use a percentage of salary. Company-side sourcing fees are scoped according to the assignment.

The goal is clarity before execution rather than surprise charges after the work begins.

NOT A JOB BOARD

More than job-board activity.

A job board tells you what has already been posted. A search strategy asks what is not being seen.

KOV's work is built around targeted research, direct communication, relevant positioning, and disciplined follow-through. That applies whether KOV is representing a professional or sourcing an executive.

## Candidate scope

Proposed URL: `/career-resources/mobility/uk-senior-specialist-worker-executive-transfers/`

The initial concept was to explain the UK Senior or Specialist Worker route for employees assigned to a UK branch of their existing employer. It must not imply eligibility, approval, employment, placement, or legal advice.

## Sources reviewed

Official GOV.UK pages were rechecked for the route overview, eligibility, fees and maintenance, required documents, applications from outside the UK, and extensions. The route replaced the Intra-company Transfer visa. Salary, work-history, stay, fee, and evidence details are time-sensitive and would require another official-source review immediately before any publication.

Direct content competitors reviewed:

- DavidsonMorris, [Senior or Specialist Worker Visa](https://www.davidsonmorris.com/senior-or-specialist-worker-visa/): covers the linked-employer transfer, eligibility, salary, overseas employment, dependants, stay limits, and route planning.
- DavidsonMorris, [Global Business Mobility Visa](https://www.davidsonmorris.com/global-business-mobility-visa/): compares the wider Global Business Mobility routes and the former Intra-company Transfer context.
- IAS, [Senior or Specialist Worker Visa](https://iasservices.org.uk/senior-or-specialist-worker-visa/): covers route eligibility, evidence, application steps, and fees. Fee details should be checked against GOV.UK.

## Qualification decision

The observed query context is primarily visa-process and immigration-advisory intent. The current candidate is framed around an executive transfer, but the research does not establish that KOV can provide the visa-specific support implied by this intent or that there is a distinct, serviceable career-planning task beyond a broader mobility resource. No first-party query evidence or keyword-volume export is available.

Therefore:

- Keep the candidate `provisional_research_candidate_not_qualified`.
- Do not write or build a visa-advisory page.
- Reconsider only if a distinct candidate-side career-planning purpose and KOV relevance can be substantiated; otherwise merge into a broader mobility resource or reject.
- Keep the candidate excluded from production, public navigation, sitemap, and internal search.

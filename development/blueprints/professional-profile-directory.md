## Research sources and limitations

- **BoardProspects** — https://www.boardprospects.com/: Presents board readiness, board-search networking, and director visibility; adjacent/direct competitor for any public executive-profile directory.
- **The Org: Stripe company profile** — https://theorg.com/org/stripe: Company profile shows organization-level details such as industry, headquarters, and employee range; not a candidate-profile directory model or evidence of consent practice.
- **LinkedIn Privacy Policy** — https://www.linkedin.com/legal/privacy-policy: Describes LinkedIn data collection, use, sharing, and member choices; reference for directory privacy review, not legal advice.
- **Data protection in the EU** — https://commission.europa.eu/law/law-topic/data-protection_en: Summarizes EU data protection framework and guidance limits; applicability requires jurisdiction-specific legal review.
- **Candidate Directory page** — profiles.html: Existing canonical candidate-directory page is robots noindex,nofollow; its data, publication intent, and consent process require review before a public replacement or parallel directory.

## Open risks and release gates

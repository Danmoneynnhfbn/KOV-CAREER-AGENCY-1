## Purpose and audience

Help senior candidates applying to UK Civil Service roles map their actual experience to the Success Profile elements and assessment methods named in a particular vacancy. Do not present the framework as a universal format or imply guaranteed outcomes.

## Source boundary

- The Civil Service Success Profiles page describes five elements and says their composition varies by role.
- The Success Profiles experience page says relevant experience is often needed for senior roles, identifies possible assessment methods, and directs candidates to the vacancy's specified elements and methods.
- Do not infer an individual campaign's process, score, or threshold from the general framework.
- Recheck official guidance and the relevant vacancy before use.

## Content outline

1. Begin with the requirements and method stated in the actual vacancy.
2. Explain relevant experience as evidence of knowledge, performance and achievements rather than tenure alone.
3. Offer a practical evidence-to-criterion matrix and accuracy checks.
4. Distinguish the five elements and explain why preparation should follow the selected element and assessment method.
5. Protect confidentiality and avoid unsupported achievements or metrics.
6. Link to related KOV resources and cite both official sources.

## Purpose and audience

Help senior finance leaders assess whether the evidence they can substantiate aligns with the remit of a specific chief executive opportunity. This page presents a decision framework, not a claim that CFO-to-CEO is a common or assured career path.

## Research basis and factual boundaries

- The 2018 U.S. SOC definitions for Financial Managers (11-3031) and Chief Executives (11-1011) were checked against locally retained BLS workbooks and existing occupation records.
- SOC wording is used only to describe occupational categories. It does not define employer requirements, readiness, appointment criteria, or transition frequency.
- Do not add salary, appointment-rate, hiring, or success claims without reliable, current, relevant evidence.

## Content outline

1. Research-stage disclosure and no-assumed-path framing.
2. Carefully attributed comparison of the two U.S. SOC definitions.
3. Candidate evidence prompts: enterprise scope, operating remit, resource decisions, leadership through others, governance, and context.
4. A five-step role-specific fit assessment.
5. Geographic and evidence limitations; existing KOV resources.

## Purpose

A practical readiness review for executives evaluating timing, positioning, and the quality of evidence before a move.

## Scope

This page is meant to support self-assessment and planning. It is not a market claim, a guarantee of access to opportunities, or a promise of any placement outcome.

## Content structure

- Transition triggers and decision logic
- Value proposition and positioning review
- Market-scan and role-fit prompts
- Network and evidence quality check
- Conditions for saying yes or no to an opportunity

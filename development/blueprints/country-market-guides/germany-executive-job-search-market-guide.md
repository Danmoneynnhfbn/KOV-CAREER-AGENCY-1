## Source boundary

- Eurostat LFSI_EMP_Q reported Germany's 2026 Q2 seasonally adjusted total employment rate for ages 20–64 as 81.5% of the total population.
- The dataset labels this employment under the resident-population concept and marks it as seasonally adjusted, not calendar adjusted.
- Eurostat's quarterly article distinguishes employment from labour-market slack.
- Do not infer vacancies, executive pay or individual appointment prospects from this aggregate observation.
- Recheck data status and metadata before publication.

## Content outline

2. Explain why employment rate is not a vacancy or executive-search measure.
3. Give a workflow for recording dataset metadata and using role-specific evidence separately.
4. State overlap, demand and serviceability gates.

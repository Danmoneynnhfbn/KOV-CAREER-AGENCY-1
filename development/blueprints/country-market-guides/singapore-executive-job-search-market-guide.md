## Source boundary

- MOM's annual 2025 report analyzes the resident labour force and includes tables on occupation, industry, income and job mobility.
- The MOM summary table defines residents as Singapore Citizens and Permanent Residents and labels its data mid-year.
- MOM's unemployment summary explains seasonal adjustment, annual averages and preliminary values.
- Do not infer executive openings, foreign-worker eligibility or pay from resident-level aggregates.
- No dynamic statistics are copied into the page.

## Content outline

1. Define resident coverage and contrast it with all-worker questions.
2. Select annual or quarterly releases and record the time period.
3. Keep seasonally adjusted, preliminary and annual-average data distinct.
4. Provide a six-step market-research workflow.
5. Link to the separate Singapore Employment Pass resource and state limits.

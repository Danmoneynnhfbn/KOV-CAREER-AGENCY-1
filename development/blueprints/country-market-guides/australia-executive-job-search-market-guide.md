## Purpose and audience

Help senior candidates researching Australia distinguish national Labour Force Survey estimates from modelled regional estimates and avoid treating broad indicators as executive vacancy evidence.

## Search intent and keyword hypotheses

- **Primary hypothesis:** Australia executive job-search market research
- **Secondary hypotheses:** Australian executive labour market; Australia executive jobs by region
- Demand, volume and competitor visibility have not been measured.

## Source boundary

- ABS Labour Force, Australia describes household-survey indicators and publishes different series presentations with release-specific notes.
- ABS methodology describes the resident civilian population aged 15 years and over and the survey design.
- The September 2026 Regional Labour Force release describes modelled SA4 estimates combining survey and administrative data using small-area estimation.
- Do not infer executive openings, pay or appointment prospects from these aggregate indicators.
- No dynamic statistics are copied into the page.

## Content outline

1. Define the survey scope and distinguish the national release.
2. Explain trend, seasonally adjusted and original series as distinct presentations.
3. Distinguish modelled SA4 estimates from direct survey estimates.
4. Provide a six-step source and geography research workflow.
5. State limits, overlap and release gates.

## Qualification and release gates

The existing executive-market resource is jurisdiction-neutral. Review its full content and Australia-specific overlap before proposing a permanent route. Complete Australia-localized SERP and first-party demand research, confirm KOV serviceability, recheck ABS sources, and obtain owner release.

Keep the draft unpublished, noindex, and excluded from public navigation, sitemap and search until all gates pass.

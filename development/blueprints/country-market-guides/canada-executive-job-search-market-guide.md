## Purpose and audience

Explain how senior candidates researching Canada can use Government of Canada Job Bank occupation, location, wage, outlook, sector, and monthly snapshot tools without treating broad indicators as executive-specific hiring evidence.

## Search intent and keyword hypotheses

- **Primary hypothesis:** Canada executive job-search market research
- **Secondary hypotheses:** Canadian executive labour market; Canada executive job prospects by region
- **Intent:** Qualitative country-market research hypothesis only. Demand has not been measured.

## Source and factual boundaries

- Job Bank's trend-analysis page was reviewed 5 November 2026; its page details show a 24 September 2026 modification date.
- The source describes research tools and update cadence. It does not provide a verified executive-vacancy count or an individual hiring forecast.
- Do not map executive titles to occupational data without checking actual duties and definitions.
- No immigration, compensation benchmark, appointment, or job-demand claim is included.

## Content outline

1. Canada-specific staging disclosure.
2. Official Job Bank tool types and geographic coverage.
3. Six steps for selecting relevant occupational, regional, and sector data.
4. A research-brief template with source-date and evidence-type distinctions.
5. Explicit limits on executive demand inference and linked current KOV resources.

## Purpose and audience

Help senior candidates researching Japan distinguish the monthly Basic and quarterly Detailed Labour Force Survey tabulations and check 2026 seasonal-adjustment and classification notices before comparing periods.

## Search intent and keyword hypotheses

- **Primary hypothesis:** Japan executive job-search market research
- **Secondary hypotheses:** Japan executive labour market; Japan labour-force statistics for senior job search
- Demand, volume and competitor visibility have not been measured.

## Source boundary

- The Statistics Bureau of Japan describes the survey's household population and monthly and quarterly tabulations.
- The monthly results page directs users to e-Stat and historical data and gives an availability notice for downloadable data.
- The Statistics Bureau announced a RegARIMA seasonal-adjustment revision applied from January 2026 results and a new industrial classification for occupation-related results.
- Do not infer executive openings, pay or appointment prospects from broad survey indicators.
- No dynamic statistics are copied into the page.

## Content outline

1. Select Basic or Detailed Tabulation for the question.
2. Record table ID, period, release date and classification.
3. Check seasonal-adjustment revisions and industrial classification changes before trend comparison.
4. Separate labour-force indicators from employer-level hiring evidence.
5. State limitations and release gates.

## Qualification and release gates

## Purpose and audience

Help senior candidates interpret UK ONS employment, unemployment, inactivity, payroll, vacancy, and earnings measures without treating aggregate data as executive-specific hiring evidence.

## Search intent and keyword hypotheses

- **Primary hypothesis:** UK executive job-search market research
- **Secondary hypotheses:** UK executive labour market; UK executive jobs by region
- **Intent:** Qualitative country-market research hypothesis only. Demand has not been measured.

## Source and factual boundaries

- The ONS UK labour-market bulletin was fetched and reviewed 5 November 2026.
- The source describes multiple indicators with differing sources, periods, and geographies and includes provisional/revision information.
- Do not infer executive openings, pay, or appointment outcomes from aggregate labour-market indicators.
- No dynamic statistics are copied into the page; recheck current releases before any publication decision.

## Content outline

1. UK scope and no executive-demand claim.
2. ONS indicator types and why they are not interchangeable.
3. Geography, period, and source-quality checklist.
4. A compact market-research brief structure for a specific executive role.
5. Limits, existing KOV resources, and ONS citation.

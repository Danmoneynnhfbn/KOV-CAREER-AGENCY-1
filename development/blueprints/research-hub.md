## Purpose and audience

A transparent index for KOV research briefs that exposes question, geography, date, method, sources, limitations, and reviewer. Current taxonomy and source notes may support reference material, but the hub must not imply a portfolio of original studies that does not yet exist.

**Audience:** Candidates, employers, and researchers seeking transparent evidence about executive careers and hiring.  
**Geographic scope:** International scope only where each research item's sources support it; otherwise label jurisdiction.

- **CIPD Reports** — https://www.cipd.org/en/knowledge/reports/: Research-library comparator covering work and working lives; does not establish a KOV search opportunity.
- **Harvard Business Review: Career Planning topic** — https://hbr.org/topic/subject/career-planning: The page aggregates career-planning content, including executive-career coverage; format benchmark only, not a measured search result or evidence of demand.
- **World Employment and Social Outlook: Trends 2025** — https://www.ilo.org/publications/flagship-reports/world-employment-and-social-outlook-trends-2025: Research-output benchmark with a report, charts, data finder, and translated summaries; does not validate any original KOV dataset.
- **Published career resources and FAQs** — resources/ (11 pages); faq/ (30 pages); glossary.html: Used to identify current internal content and overlap; not evidence of user demand.

## Purpose and audience

Offer executives a bounded set of questions to clarify one potential board or non-executive director opportunity before deciding whether to proceed. This is not a legal, fiduciary, suitability, compensation, or acceptance assessment.

**Audience:** Executives considering an appointment and seeking to clarify the stated remit and context.  
**Geographic scope:** Questions are general prompts. Terminology, governance codes, duties, and appointment processes vary. The UK Corporate Governance Code is cited only as a named UK example.

## Page structure

1. Identify the entity, jurisdiction, title, appointing process, and written remit.
2. Provide neutral discussion prompts about the remit, organization, board/committee context, practical arrangements, information, and terms.
3. Invite the reader to compare the stated remit with their own verifiable experience and constraints.
4. Describe the UK Code’s named sections only as a bounded UK reference.
5. Suggest pausing to clarify missing or conflicting information and refer legal, financial, tax, regulatory, and disclosure questions to qualified advisers.
6. Link to the existing KOV board-transition resource and the staging-only board CV guide.

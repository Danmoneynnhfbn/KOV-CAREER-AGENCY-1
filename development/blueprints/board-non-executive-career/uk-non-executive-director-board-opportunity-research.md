## Purpose and audience

Help experienced executives and directors use UK-listed-company governance disclosures to understand board composition and appointment processes without treating a skills disclosure as a live vacancy or fit guarantee.

## Search intent and keyword hypotheses

- **Primary hypothesis:** UK non-executive director board opportunity research
- **Secondary hypotheses:** UK board succession disclosure; researching non-executive director appointments
- Demand, volume and competitor visibility have not been measured.

## Source boundary

- FRC UK Corporate Governance Code 2024 Principle K addresses board skills, experience and knowledge.
- Provision 17 covers nomination committee and succession responsibilities.
- Provision 20 says open advertising and/or external search consultancy should generally be used for chair and NED appointments.
- Provisions 21–22 address board review and development.
- Do not infer a live vacancy, private search, appointment probability or legal duty for an out-of-scope company.

## Content outline

1. Establish the company's stated reporting framework.
2. Read board composition, biographies, committee roles and succession disclosures.
3. Separate public process descriptions from a current opportunity.
4. Build a research matrix matching disclosures to candidate evidence.
5. Link to the separate board-CV and opportunity-due-diligence resources.

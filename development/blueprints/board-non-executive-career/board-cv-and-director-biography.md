## Purpose and audience

Help executives review and substantiate governance-relevant experience for a specified board CV or director biography request. The page is an evidence-audit aid, not a standardized format or a promise of appointment.

- **Audience:** Executives considering a first or subsequent board role.
- **Geographic scope:** General editorial guidance with explicit jurisdiction limits. The UK Corporate Governance Code is cited only as a UK-specific context.

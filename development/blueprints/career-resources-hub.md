## Purpose and audience

A navigational index that organizes KOV's existing candidate-facing career resources and only later adds individually researched guides. It should not duplicate the articles themselves or imply all listed occupations/markets are KOV services.

**Audience:** Senior and experienced professionals browsing KOV's published career information.  
**Geographic scope:** English-language online information; country-specific applicability is not implied.

## Search intent and demand evidence

- **Primary query hypothesis:** executive career resources
- **Secondary query hypotheses:** career resources for executives, executive career planning, senior professional career guide
- **Intent:** Informational/navigation hub; terms are unvalidated query hypotheses.
- **Measured demand:** Not available; no volume or ranking claim is made.
- **Competitor/reference pages:** https://hbr.org/topic/subject/career-planning, https://hbr.org/2026/08/the-predictable-executive-career-arc-is-over, https://www.onetonline.org/, https://www.indeed.com/career-advice
- **SERP positions:** Not measured; direct page review is not a SERP study.

## Existing-site overlap and differentiation

Current materials are already spread across 11 resource pages, 30 FAQs, a glossary, service pages, and role pages. The hub is differentiated only as a curated index; it must link to existing URLs first and not create near-duplicate copy.

## Page-specific information architecture

- Purpose and audience
- Existing resources grouped by researched user task
- Links to current role/market reference pages with scope labels
- Editorial dates and review policy
- How to distinguish guidance from KOV services

## Research sources and limitations

- **Harvard Business Review: Career Planning topic** — https://hbr.org/topic/subject/career-planning: The page aggregates career-planning content, including executive-career coverage; format benchmark only, not a measured search result or evidence of demand.
- **The Predictable Executive Career Arc Is Over** — https://hbr.org/2026/08/the-predictable-executive-career-arc-is-over: Discusses senior leaders' career planning amid longer working lives and change; topic reference only, not evidence of KOV serviceability or search demand.
- **O*NET OnLine** — https://www.onetonline.org/: Describes its occupational exploration and analysis tools; U.S.-focused comparator, not an executive-career service or global resource.
- **Indeed Career Advice** — https://www.indeed.com/career-advice: Potential broad career-advice comparator; page contents and current SERP placement not verified.
- **Published career resources and FAQs** — resources/ (11 pages); faq/ (30 pages); glossary.html: Used to identify current internal content and overlap; not evidence of user demand.

## Open risks and release gates

- No first-party search, traffic, or user-task data was available to validate taxonomy or keyword demand.
- Do not add unresearched guides or imply that taxonomy coverage equals KOV serviceability.

Keep the preview noindex and out of public navigation, sitemap, and search until page-specific content, QA, and publication approval are complete.

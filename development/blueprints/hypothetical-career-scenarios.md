# Hypothetical Career-Planning Scenario Collection

- **Collection:** `hypothetical-career-scenarios`
- **Staging route:** `/directory/resources/hypothetical-career-scenarios/`
- **Status:** Fictional editorial prototypes only; not eligible for the 577-page researched-content count.
- **Production decision:** None. No production routes, indexation, public navigation, or search inclusion is approved.

## Purpose and audience

Provide twelve short fictional planning exercises that help readers identify questions they may want to investigate about a career decision. They are discussion prompts, not reports of services delivered or results obtained.

## Mandatory disclosure and boundaries

Each scenario page must prominently state that it is fictional and not based on a real KOV client, engagement, testimonial, placement, or outcome. The collection landing page repeats this disclosure. Do not remove or obscure these statements.

Do not invent real people, employers, quotes, client histories, credentials, metrics, placement results, salary changes, or service outcomes. Avoid presenting any scenario as a case history. Keep every page `noindex, nofollow`, without a public canonical, stylesheet, sitemap entry, public-search entry, or production navigation.

## Page structure

1. Fiction disclosure immediately before the single H1.
2. A brief invented situation with no identifying real-world entities or fabricated performance facts.
3. Questions the hypothetical person could investigate.
4. A neutral planning exercise that separates verified personal evidence from unresolved questions.
5. A reminder that the scenario is invented and is not evidence of KOV service or outcomes.

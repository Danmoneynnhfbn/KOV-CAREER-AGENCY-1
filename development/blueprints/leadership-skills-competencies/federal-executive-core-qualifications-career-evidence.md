## Purpose and audience

Help senior U.S. public-sector executives review evidence against OPM's five current SES Executive Core Qualifications and translate relevant experience into an employer-specific narrative when exploring another executive role.

The page does not claim that ECQs are a private-sector standard or that the framework makes federal and private-sector qualifications equivalent. It provides no legal, ethics, SES-eligibility, or classified-information advice.

## Source and factual limits

- OPM's ECQ page was retrieved 5 November 2026 and states the framework was updated 1 October 2025.
- OPM identifies five ECQs and explains their federal SES and agency context.
- Do not imply that a private-sector employer adopts the framework, that public-service competencies directly equal private-sector criteria, or that the page determines candidate eligibility.

## Content outline

1. Federal scope and non-equivalence disclosure.
2. The five ECQ areas, summarized and attributed to OPM.
3. Evidence-translation prompts for each area, with context and attribution limits.
4. A five-step worksheet for mapping evidence to a specific employer's stated requirements.
5. Limitations, related existing resources, and OPM citation.

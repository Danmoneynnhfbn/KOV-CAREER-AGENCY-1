## Purpose and audience

Provide a navigational landing page for the 2018 U.S. SOC major group 55-0000, Military Specific Occupations, and link only to KOV role pages whose mapping to this group has been individually checked. Do not copy the full SOC taxonomy or make a placement/service claim.

**Audience:** People browsing KOV's role directory who need to locate existing roles within the Military Specific Occupations classification group; whether this group fits KOV's candidate-service audience is unverified.  
**Geographic scope:** The 2018 SOC is a U.S. occupational classification. Any English-language page must label that scope and must not imply the taxonomy is universal or equivalent to other national systems.

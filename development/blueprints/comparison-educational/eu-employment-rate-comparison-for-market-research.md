## Purpose and audience

Compare Germany, the Netherlands and the EU-27 aggregate using one quarter, age band, unit and seasonal-adjustment definition. Teach readers to distinguish employment rate from labour-market slack and executive demand.

## Search intent and keyword hypotheses

- **Primary hypothesis:** compare EU employment rates for market research
- **Secondary hypotheses:** Germany Netherlands employment rate comparison; Eurostat employment rate 2026 Q2
- Demand, volume and competitor visibility have not been measured.

## Source boundary

- Eurostat LFSI_EMP_Q data updated 26 October 2026 reports values for 2026 Q2.
- All observations use ages 20–64, total employment under the LFS resident-population concept, percentage of total population, seasonally adjusted and not calendar adjusted.
- Values: Netherlands 83.2%; Germany 81.5%; EU-27 aggregate 76.4%.
- Eurostat glossary defines employment rate relative to a comparable total population and labour-market slack against the extended labour force.
- Do not infer vacancies, executive pay or individual appointment prospects from these aggregate indicators.

## Content outline

1. Provide a same-period three-row comparison table.
2. Explain the numerator, denominator, age band and adjustment.
3. Distinguish employment from labour-market slack.
4. Provide a market-screen workflow and explicit limitations.
5. Link to the Germany-specific research page.

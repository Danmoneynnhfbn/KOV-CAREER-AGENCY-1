## Purpose and audience

Help current U.S. federal employees identify when private-sector job-search contact may call for agency ethics review and prepare questions for their own ethics office. It is not legal advice or a general government-career overview.

**Audience:** Current or recently departed senior U.S. federal employees considering private employers, contractors, or consulting.  
**Geographic scope:** United States federal ethics context; agency-specific and circumstance-specific rules must be verified.

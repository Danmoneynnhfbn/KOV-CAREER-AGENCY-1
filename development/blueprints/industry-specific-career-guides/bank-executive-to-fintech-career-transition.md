## Purpose and audience

Help a bank leader assess and explain how experience in regulated banking, third-party risk, customer-facing arrangements, and governance maps to a fintech role without implying that fintech is unregulated.

**Audience:** Senior banking executives considering fintech companies or financial-technology partnership roles.
**Geographic scope:** Lead with U.S. banking context; clearly flag jurisdiction and employer-model variation.

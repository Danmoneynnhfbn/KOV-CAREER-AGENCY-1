## Purpose and audience

Provide a U.S.-specific career-evidence map for senior leaders exploring operating roles with organizations participating in Medicare. Focus on the organization/service model, individual decision rights, oversight interfaces, change evidence, and careful transfer of experience.

The topic is deliberately narrower than KOV's existing Healthcare and Social Care industry page. That existing route already covers broad candidate positioning and links to role-industry resources. This draft must stay limited to Medicare provider/supplier context and a role-evidence exercise; do not reproduce the general industry page.

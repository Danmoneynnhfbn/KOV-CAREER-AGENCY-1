## Purpose and audience

Give senior candidates a U.S.-specific preparation approach for federal structured interviews as described by OPM. The guide focuses on truthful evidence, competency alignment, and the difference between past-behavior and hypothetical questions.

It does not predict questions, claim every federal vacancy uses the method, or generalize the process to all executive interviews.

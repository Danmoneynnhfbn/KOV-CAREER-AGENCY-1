## Purpose and audience

Help senior U.S. federal candidates identify the relevant official OPM pay-table source and keep that reference distinct from private-sector offer terms. No pay values, market benchmarks, projections, or compensation-equivalence ratios are published.

## Search intent and keyword hypotheses

- **Primary hypothesis:** U.S. federal executive pay tables career comparison
- **Secondary hypotheses:** OPM executive pay table; federal executive compensation comparison
- **Intent:** Qualitative career-planning hypothesis only. Search demand has not been measured.

## Source and factual boundaries

- OPM's 2026 Executive and Senior Level index was fetched 5 November 2026 and lists several distinct table files.
- The page is an index, not evidence that a specific table applies to a particular person or role.
- Do not infer federal/private-sector equivalence, individual pay eligibility, market rates, or compensation value.
- Do not provide tax, legal, or financial advice or calculate compensation components.

## Content outline

1. Research-stage disclosure and U.S. federal scope.
2. OPM's multiple-table index and the need to verify the relevant pay system with the employer.
3. A like-for-like comparison checklist for pay period, location, cash components, role scope, and offer terms.
4. Questions for the employing agency and qualified advisers.
5. Explicit exclusion of benchmarks and salary figures; cited OPM source.

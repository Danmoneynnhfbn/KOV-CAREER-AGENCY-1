# Occupation Classification Route Research Batch

**Research date:** 2026-11-04  
**Status:** Twenty-three existing SOC major-group link targets have individual provisional route screens. As of 2026-11-05, Management Occupations is qualified for unpublished staging development as a U.S.-specific internal navigation resource; the other 22 remain provisional. No public-query volume or ranking claim is made.

## Follow-up evidence check — 2026-11-04

- The local 2018 SOC structure and definitions workbooks remain internally consistent for the codes and titles recorded in this batch. They are U.S.-specific reference material, not proof of KOV service scope or candidate demand.
- Attempts to retrieve the BLS 2018 SOC landing page and its direct-match title file returned HTTP 403. O*NET's official family page was reachable but did not provide enough candidate-specific detail in the fetched text to close the role-mapping gate.
- Representative Bing queries for major-group topics returned unrelated results (including stock-photo pages and generic search/service pages). These results are not a usable SERP sample; no result count, ranking, competitor set, or query-demand conclusion is recorded.
- The internal link audit remains evidence of a site-navigation path: each missing SOC target occurs 105 times across 35 pages. It does not show that the corresponding occupation groups fit KOV's audience or that each deserves an independent page.

**Qualification outcome:** No SOC candidate is advanced. Before staging implementation, each route still needs an evidence-based decision on audience fit and purpose, verified mapping to existing role pages or an explicit no-mapping rationale, full page-overlap review, and reliable candidate-specific competitor/search evidence. The 23 candidates remain unpublished and excluded.

## Follow-up evidence and staging decision — 2026-11-05

- The O*NET Chief Executives (11-1011.00) profile was reviewed directly. It includes CEO, CFO, CIO, COO, and CTO among sample reported job titles; the staging page links only to KOV role resources for those titles and does not claim that titles or duties are interchangeable.
- Two Google searches localized to the United States were reviewed: `"Management Occupations" "11-0000"` surfaced BLS and O*NET occupational references; `"management occupations" career directory` surfaced BLS Occupational Outlook Handbook, Indeed degree-focused career guidance, workforce resources, and university content. These qualitative screens support a classification-navigation purpose, not measured demand or stable ranking claims.
- Indeed's “21 Jobs You Can Get With a Business Management Degree” (updated 2026-06-15) was reviewed as adjacent content. Its degree-oriented audience and broad career list differ from a U.S. SOC index that routes visitors to existing executive role pages.
- The exact proposed route was previously found in 105 internal links across 35 pages. Combined with the user's approved classification architecture, this supports development as an internal navigation resource, not a standalone demand or traffic forecast.
- A substantive, unstyled, noindex page is built at `staging/roles/management-occupations/index.html`. It is excluded from production sitemap and public search. Search metrics remain unavailable and are not claimed.

**Qualification outcome:** One candidate is qualified for staging development; the other 22 remain provisional pending their own role mapping, overlap, audience, and evidence review. Staging approval is not production or publication approval.

SEARCH ACTIVITY TIMELINE

A route from career objective to informed decisions.

Open a stage to see the kind of work it can contain. The agreed scope determines which activities apply.

  1. 01Audit

    Clarify career direction, constraints, priorities, and the evidence available to support the search.

  2. 02Position

    Align resume, LinkedIn, and professional messaging with the agreed target without inventing achievements.

  3. 03Research

    Explore target industries, organization types, geographies, and relevant market signals.

  4. 04Target

    Prioritize organizations and opportunities against criteria agreed with the professional.

  5. 05Apply

    Prepare or execute relevant applications according to the engagement and approval process.

  6. 06Outreach

    Coordinate personalized recruiter or hiring-manager contact where appropriate.

  7. 07Follow-up

    Track approved activity, responses, follow-up timing, and next actions.

  8. 08Interview

    Prepare for role-specific conversations using research, positioning, practice, and feedback.

  9. 09Negotiation

    Consider terms and negotiation strategy if an offer is made. No offer or outcome is implied.

SEARCH ACTIVITY

Connect research to relevant action.

Each activity is defined by the agreed target, scope, and approval process.

Research and targeting

Assess organization type, market context, role mandate, and relevant decision-makers against the criteria agreed with you.

Deliverables may include target priorities, research notes, and questions to resolve before outreach.

Positioning

Connect verified experience to the requirements of the target role across your resume, LinkedIn profile, and professional messaging.

Factual content is reviewed with you before publication or use.

Applications and outreach

Prepare or coordinate suitable applications and personalized recruiter or hiring-manager contact where included in the agreed scope.

External activity follows your approval and communication preferences.

Interview and offer preparation

Use role and organization research to prepare for conversations, evaluate offer components, and plan considered next steps.

Support is defined in the engagement; employers make independent hiring decisions.

WHAT YOU CAN REVIEW

Keep activity, approvals, and next steps connected.

Depending on the engagement, you can review target roles, proposed applications, outreach status, responses, follow-ups, and preparation tasks through the agreed reporting process.

RESEARCH / METHODS & SOURCES

Research hub

A transparent home for research about careers and work. Every published item should make its question, scope, sources, date, and limitations clear enough for readers to assess what the evidence can support.

Publication status

No original KOV labor-market research reports are approved for publication at this time. This page does not present candidate screens, internal planning registers, or source lists as published studies or proprietary market data.

Existing KOV career resources are available in the career outcomes overview and the market exploration guide. These are informational pages, not original quantitative research.

What a KOV research brief must show

A research page is useful only when its evidence can be interpreted in context. Each future brief will identify:

Question and audience

The specific question being examined, who the findings may help, and what the brief does not attempt to answer.

Geography and period

The countries, regions, or jurisdictions covered and the dates represented by the underlying evidence.

Sources and method

Where information came from, how it was selected or analyzed, and whether the source is primary, official, or interpretive.

Limitations and review

Known gaps, uncertainty, reviewer, publication date, and what would trigger a correction or update.

External research and reference sources

These independent publishers and tools are useful starting points for readers. Their inclusion is not an endorsement, and their findings should not be attributed to KOV.

International Labour Organization

The ILO's World Employment and Social Outlook: Trends 2025 is a global employment report. Its scope and reference period should be checked before applying any finding to an individual executive or local market.

Read the ILO report

Chartered Institute of Personnel and Development

CIPD's research library publishes reports on work and working lives. Individual reports have their own methods, populations, and geographic limits.

Browse CIPD reports

O*NET OnLine

O*NET is a U.S.-focused occupational exploration and analysis resource. Its classifications should not be treated as a universal role taxonomy.

Explore O*NET OnLine

Harvard Business Review career planning

This editorial topic collection is a source of career-planning perspectives, not an official statistical dataset or a measure of job-market demand.

Browse career-planning articles

Corrections and updates

Before publication, each report will be checked against its cited sources and reviewed for scope, date, and unsupported conclusions. When a material error is identified, the report should be corrected with a note describing the change; where evidence has become stale, the page should be reviewed or clearly marked as historical.

Research planning is ongoing. The Career Resources Hub organizes currently published career information separately from future research reports.

Evidence boundary: No search-volume, ranking, hiring-demand, salary, or placement claims are made on this page. Future KOV research findings will be published only after the underlying evidence and methodology are complete and reviewed.

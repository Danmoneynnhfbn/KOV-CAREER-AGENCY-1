CONFIDENTIALITY

Approach professionals with context and care.

Passive executives may be currently employed and not actively considering a move. KOV agrees the search brief, outreach boundaries, message, and approval process with the client before contact. The approach is tailored to the individual context and avoids unnecessary disclosure.

Confidentiality practices are defined for each engagement. This description is not a legal or security guarantee.

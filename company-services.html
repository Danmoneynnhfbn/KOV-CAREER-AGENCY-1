THE PROBLEM

The best candidate is not always searching.

Public job postings capture active applicants. Executive talent often requires a different approach.

KOV helps companies identify people based on where they work, what they have built, the environments they have operated in, and how their background aligns with the role.

The objective is not a larger list. The objective is a more relevant list.

THE KOV SOURCING METHOD

We map the market before we approach the person.

  1. 01 · DEFINE

    Clarify the role, success profile, seniority, geography, target companies, and search constraints.

  2. 02 · MAP

    Identify relevant companies, leadership teams, career histories, professional networks, and potential talent pools.

  3. 03 · RESEARCH

    Use publicly available research, company information, professional signals, industry intelligence, and other appropriate sources to understand candidate fit.

  4. 04 · PRIORITIZE

    Separate broad matches from the people who genuinely fit the brief.

  5. 05 · APPROACH

    Engage relevant senior professionals through credible, personalized, and discreet outreach.

  6. 06 · PRESENT

    Deliver relevant candidate information according to the agreed engagement and communication process.

PASSIVE TALENT INTELLIGENCE

Research before outreach.

KOV's sourcing practice is research-led. Depending on the engagement, research may include company announcements, public filings, industry intelligence, professional history, target-company mapping, leadership-team analysis, professional networks, warm introductions, market signals, and publicly available information.

The goal is to understand the market before making contact.

CONFIDENTIALITY

Executive outreach should be discreet.

Senior professionals are often already employed. That means outreach has to be handled carefully.

KOV approaches passive candidates with professionalism and discretion, with the aim of protecting the candidate experience and the client's reputation throughout the process.

GLOBAL REACH

One search. Multiple markets.

KOV works across North America, Europe, and Asia, supporting companies that need access to senior talent beyond a single local market.

International searches require more than geography. They require market understanding, time-zone awareness, context, and careful outreach.

ENGAGEMENT

Every search starts with scope.

KOV does not force every executive search into a standard package. Fees are discussed according to role level, search scope, geography, timeline, research depth, engagement type, and whether the requirement is a focused single-role search or a broader pipeline engagement.

The scope and fee are discussed and agreed in writing before work begins.

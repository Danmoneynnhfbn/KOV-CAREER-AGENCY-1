Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions from professionals.

Who is KOV for?

KOV works with senior and experienced professionals, including executives, specialists, and leaders exploring a confidential move.

What does KOV actually do?

KOV researches target employers, prepares positioning, supports direct outreach, and helps professionals navigate search and interview conversations.

Is this confidential?

Yes. The work is designed to be discreet, with agreed scope and communication boundaries set before outreach begins.

Do you guarantee interviews or jobs?

No. KOV does not guarantee interviews, offers, employment, or timelines. Employers make hiring decisions.

How do fees work?

Fees depend on the scope agreed before work begins and are confirmed in writing before any work starts.

What is the next step?

Start with a focused inquiry and KOV will discuss fit, scope, and the services most relevant to your situation.

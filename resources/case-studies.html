DEFINE THE CONTEXT

Start with the role, market or question—not assumptions.

A focused search begins with the professional's objectives, constraints and evidence. KOV can then define target criteria, research relevant organizations, strengthen positioning and coordinate approved activity through the agreed scope.

KOV connects career positioning, market research and agreed search activity so senior professionals can make informed decisions at each stage. The first step is to make the decision criteria clear enough to guide useful research and practical next actions.

  • Define target roles, organizations and markets
  • Connect verified experience to the target mandate
  • Coordinate applications and outreach with approval

COMMON QUESTIONS

Before you start a conversation.

What should I share to start?

KOV connects career positioning, market research and agreed search activity so senior professionals can make informed decisions at each stage. Start with the role or question, relevant markets and practical constraints; share sensitive details only when they are needed.

Does this service guarantee a job, hire or timeline?

No. KOV can carry out only the activities agreed in writing. Employers control hiring decisions, and market conditions and timing remain outside KOV’s control.

How are fees handled?

Fees are discussed directly by message, alongside scope and deliverables, and agreed in writing before work begins.

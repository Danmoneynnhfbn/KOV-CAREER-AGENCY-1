Individual Career Services FAQ, Part 2

Questions and answers from KOV Career Agency. Scope is agreed before work begins, fees are confirmed in writing, and no employment, interview or hiring outcome is promised. Contact: daniel@kovcareeragency.org.

Can Personal Career Website be combined with Private Career Representation?

Private Career Representation and Individual Career Services are separate offerings, each discussed on its own scope and terms. Separate document or profile work is scoped as an Individual Career Service.

Can I live abroad and still get Personal Career Website from KOV?

Yes in most cases. KOV works remotely with professionals in global markets; availability depends on location and engagement fit, so contact KOV to confirm.

Does Personal Career Website guarantee interviews or a job?

No. KOV does not promise employment, interview, hiring or timeline outcomes, because employers make hiring decisions.

How do I request Personal Career Website?

Send an inquiry through the contact page or email daniel@kovcareeragency.org. Individual Career Services are available through inquiry and quotation.

Is Personal Career Website confidential?

Confidentiality is the default at KOV, and your information is used only as agreed for the work.

Can I get Personal Career Website without Private Career Representation?

Yes. The seven Individual Career Services are offered separately and can be requested on their own.

How are revisions handled for Personal Career Website?

Work is reviewed and finalised within the agreed revision process, which is confirmed as part of the scope and quotation.

What is the first step for Personal Career Website?

The first step is understanding your objective, then confirming the right scope before a tailored quotation is provided.

Is Personal Career Website suitable for executives?

KOV works with senior and executive professionals. Suitability for your situation is discussed when you enquire.

Is Personal Career Website suitable for mid-career professionals?

It can be, depending on your goals. Fit and scope are discussed before work begins.

Is Personal Career Website useful for career changers?

It can help present transferable experience clearly for a new direction. Scope is tailored to your situation.

Does Personal Career Website help with remote job searches?

The work can be shaped around the roles and markets you are targeting, including remote or international ones. Scope is agreed in advance.

Does Personal Career Website help with international job searches?

It can be adapted to the market you are targeting. Confirm right-to-work and local requirements for each opportunity yourself.

What is the difference between Personal Career Website and Private Career Representation?

Personal Career Website is a standalone service quoted on inquiry. Private Career Representation is KOV's primary managed service covering strategy, research, positioning and outreach.

Do I work directly with Daniel for Personal Career Website?

KOV is founder-led, and Daniel Ade is directly involved, giving you one accountable point of contact.

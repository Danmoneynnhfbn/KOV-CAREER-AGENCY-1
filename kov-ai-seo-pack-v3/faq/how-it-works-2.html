How KOV Works FAQ, Part 2

Questions and answers from KOV Career Agency. Scope is agreed before work begins, fees are confirmed in writing, and no employment, interview or hiring outcome is promised. Contact: daniel@kovcareeragency.org.

Will I be consulted about approval preferences?

KOV works with you directly. Responsibilities, approvals and communication are agreed up front, and you keep control of career decisions.

Where does approval preferences begin?

It begins with understanding your goals, background and target direction. Applications are coordinated in line with your approval preferences, so you decide how much you review before anything is sent.

Can I follow how approval preferences is progressing?

KOV coordinates responses and next steps with you, and the way you are kept informed is agreed when scope is set.

Does approval preferences guarantee results?

No. KOV does not promise employment, interview, hiring or timeline outcomes, because employers make hiring decisions.

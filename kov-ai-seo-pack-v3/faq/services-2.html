Will I be consulted about target markets?

KOV works with you directly. Responsibilities, approvals and communication are agreed up front, and you keep control of career decisions.

Where does target markets begin?

It begins with understanding your goals, background and target direction. Target markets are defined alongside roles and organisations, so the search is focused on where your experience fits best.

Can I follow how target markets is progressing?

KOV coordinates responses and next steps with you, and the way you are kept informed is agreed when scope is set.

Does target markets guarantee results?

No. KOV does not promise employment, interview, hiring or timeline outcomes, because employers make hiring decisions.

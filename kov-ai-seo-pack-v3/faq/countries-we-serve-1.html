Countries We Serve FAQ, Part 1 (Afghanistan to Central African Republic)

Questions and answers from KOV Career Agency. Scope is agreed before work begins, fees are confirmed in writing, and no employment, interview or hiring outcome is promised. Contact: daniel@kovcareeragency.org.

Does KOV Career Agency work with professionals in Afghanistan?

KOV works remotely with professionals in global markets, including Afghanistan. Service availability depends on location and engagement fit, so contact KOV to confirm availability for your goals.

Can I work with KOV from Afghanistan while targeting jobs in another country?

Yes in most cases, because KOV works remotely. Support for multi-country opportunities is discussed individually, and you should confirm right-to-work requirements for each country you target.

Can KOV support an executive job search in Afghanistan, including Kabul, Kandahar, Herat?

KOV can discuss research-led career support for professionals targeting roles in Afghanistan, including Kabul, Kandahar, Herat. The market pages describe candidate career-search context only, with no local office or guaranteed market access, and no job is guaranteed.

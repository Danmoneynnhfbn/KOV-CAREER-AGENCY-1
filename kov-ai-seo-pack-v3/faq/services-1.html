Private Career Representation FAQ, Part 1

Questions and answers from KOV Career Agency. Scope is agreed before work begins, fees are confirmed in writing, and no employment, interview or hiring outcome is promised. Contact: daniel@kovcareeragency.org.

What is Assessment and Fit?

Assessment and Fit means discussing your objectives, constraints and the support you want, reviewing your background and target direction, checking whether representation suits you, and agreeing scope, responsibilities, approvals and communication.

Why does Assessment and Fit matter in a career search?

Assessment and Fit means discussing your objectives, constraints and the support you want, reviewing your background and target direction, checking whether representation suits you, and agreeing scope, responsibilities, approvals and communication. A clear approach to this makes the rest of the search more focused.

How does KOV handle Assessment and Fit?

Assessment and Fit means discussing your objectives, constraints and the support you want, reviewing your background and target direction, checking whether representation suits you, and agreeing scope, responsibilities, approvals and communication. Scope is agreed before work begins.

Is Assessment and Fit part of Private Career Representation?

Assessment and Fit means discussing your objectives, constraints and the support you want, reviewing your background and target direction, checking whether representation suits you, and agreeing scope, responsibilities, approvals and communication. The exact scope for your case is agreed before work begins.

Will I be consulted about Assessment and Fit?

KOV works with you directly. Responsibilities, approvals and communication are agreed up front, and you keep control of career decisions.

Where does Assessment and Fit begin?

It begins with understanding your goals, background and target direction. Assessment and Fit means discussing your objectives, constraints and the support you want, reviewing your background and target direction, checking whether representation suits you, and agreeing scope, responsibilities, approvals and communication.

Can I follow how Assessment and Fit is progressing?

KOV coordinates responses and next steps with you, and the way you are kept informed is agreed when scope is set.

Does Assessment and Fit guarantee results?

No. KOV does not promise employment, interview, hiring or timeline outcomes, because employers make hiring decisions.

How KOV Works FAQ, Part 1

Questions and answers from KOV Career Agency. Scope is agreed before work begins, fees are confirmed in writing, and no employment, interview or hiring outcome is promised. Contact: daniel@kovcareeragency.org.

What is Onboarding?

In Onboarding, goals, target roles and search strategy are defined, and the resume and LinkedIn profile are rewritten.

Why does Onboarding matter in a career search?

In Onboarding, goals, target roles and search strategy are defined, and the resume and LinkedIn profile are rewritten. A clear approach to this makes the rest of the search more focused.

How does KOV handle Onboarding?

In Onboarding, goals, target roles and search strategy are defined, and the resume and LinkedIn profile are rewritten. Scope is agreed before work begins.

Is Onboarding part of KOV's six-step process?

In Onboarding, goals, target roles and search strategy are defined, and the resume and LinkedIn profile are rewritten. The exact scope for your case is agreed before work begins.

Will I be consulted about Onboarding?

KOV works with you directly. Responsibilities, approvals and communication are agreed up front, and you keep control of career decisions.

Where does Onboarding begin?

It begins with understanding your goals, background and target direction. In Onboarding, goals, target roles and search strategy are defined, and the resume and LinkedIn profile are rewritten.

Can I follow how Onboarding is progressing?

KOV coordinates responses and next steps with you, and the way you are kept informed is agreed when scope is set.

Does Onboarding guarantee results?

No. KOV does not promise employment, interview, hiring or timeline outcomes, because employers make hiring decisions.

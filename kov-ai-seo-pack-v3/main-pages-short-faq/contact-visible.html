Frequently Asked Questions

What should I know about emailing KOV?

You can email daniel@kovcareeragency.org with a short summary of your background and goals.

What should I know about the direct inquiry?

The direct inquiry route reaches Daniel, who works directly with clients.

What should I know about what to include in an enquiry?

A short summary of your background, target roles, location and what kind of support you want helps KOV respond usefully.

What should I know about an enquiry about Private Career Representation?

Representation is not offered automatically. Suitability is assessed during Assessment and Fit.

What should I know about an enquiry about Individual Career Services?

Individual Career Services are available through inquiry and quotation, with scope agreed before work begins.

What should I know about a confidential enquiry?

Confidentiality is the default, so you can raise sensitive circumstances in your enquiry.

What should I know about an enquiry from abroad?

KOV works remotely with professionals in global markets. Contact KOV to confirm availability for your location.

What should I know about a multi-country enquiry?

Support for multi-country opportunities is discussed individually.

What should I know about right-to-work questions?

Confirm right-to-work and local requirements for each opportunity yourself.

What should I know about fee questions in an enquiry?

Enquiries are free, and fees are discussed once scope is understood and confirmed in writing before work begins.

What should I know about a tailored quotation after enquiry?

A tailored quotation is provided before work starts.

What should I know about discussing scope and fit?

Scope and fit are discussed before work begins.

What should I know about the get started page?

The Get Started page is the starting point for inquiries.

What should I know about the inquiry subject line?

KOV's direct inquiry link uses a subject line such as Inquiry — Private Representation; any clear subject works.

What should I know about asking for help choosing a service?

If you are unsure where to start, ask KOV to help you choose a starting point.

What should I know about asking about timelines?

Timing is discussed individually. No hiring or timeline outcome is promised.

What should I know about asking about Daniel's availability?

The number of active searches is capped, so ask about availability when you enquire.

What should I know about the privacy notice?

KOV's Privacy Notice explains how information is handled; review it before sending sensitive details.

What should I know about sending your resume with an enquiry?

You can mention your resume or LinkedIn profile in your enquiry; KOV will say what it needs during the initial review.

What should I know about a follow-up after your enquiry?

If you do not hear back, you can send a short follow-up to the same email address.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who is KOV the right fit for?

KOV works with senior and experienced professionals who want private career representation or specific individual career services. Suitability is assessed during Assessment and Fit.

Is a reverse recruiter the same as a career coach?

No. A career coach mainly advises and guides. A reverse recruiter also carries out target-company research, outreach and follow-up on the candidate's behalf.

How long does an executive job search take?

Executive searches commonly take several months, depending on level, sector, location and market conditions.

Are fees agreed before work begins?

Yes. Terms are confirmed in writing before any work starts.

Can Strategy Directors work with KOV Career Agency?

KOV works for the candidate, never for corporate HR.

KOV offers support for specific career needs through separate Individual Career Services such as CV writing, LinkedIn optimization, portfolios and coaching.

Work starts with understanding your goals, the relevant market, target organisations and the people who make hiring decisions, and outreach follows with context.

What does KOV mean by search intelligence?

Search intelligence means research comes first: understanding the target before any outreach is considered.

What does KOV mean by having one accountable operator?

Daniel Ade is directly involved, giving you one accountable point of contact.

What does KOV mean by clear scope before work begins?

Scope is clarified and agreed in writing before work begins.

What does KOV mean by going beyond job-board activity?

KOV's work is more than job-board activity: research, targeting, positioning, human outreach and follow-up.

What does KOV mean by the split between private representation and individual services?

Private Career Representation and Individual Career Services are clearly separated, each discussed on its own scope and terms.

What does KOV mean by the boutique model?

KOV is deliberately small and is not built to become a large-volume career service.

What does KOV mean by founder-led work?

KOV is founder-led, with no junior hand-off and no offshore desk.

What does KOV mean by flexible, scoped fees?

Fees are agreed individually based on requirements and confirmed in writing before work begins.

What does KOV mean by proactive career support?

KOV focuses on uncovering opportunities that are not publicly posted rather than only reviewing listings.

What does KOV mean by division of responsibility?

KOV handles execution for senior professionals, who retain control of career decisions.

What does KOV mean by target organisations and decision-makers?

KOV researches target organisations and the people who make hiring decisions before outreach.

What does KOV mean by tracked outreach?

Outreach follows research with context, and the process is tracked.

What does KOV mean by opportunities that are not publicly posted?

Some opportunities are uncovered through recruiter relationships, hiring-manager conversations or warm introductions rather than job boards.

What does KOV mean by confidentiality by default?

Confidentiality is treated as the default.

What does KOV mean by a limited number of active searches?

The number of active searches is deliberately capped so each client receives focused attention.

What does KOV mean by the absence of junior hand-offs?

What does KOV mean by human outreach and follow-up?

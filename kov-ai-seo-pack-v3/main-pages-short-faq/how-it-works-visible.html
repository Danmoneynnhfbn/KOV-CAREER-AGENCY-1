Frequently Asked Questions

What is Onboarding?

In Onboarding, goals, target roles and search strategy are defined, and the resume and LinkedIn profile are rewritten.

What is Job Applications?

Targeted applications are coordinated in line with the agreed strategy and your approval preferences.

What is Outreach?

In Outreach, for each role applied to, the recruiter and hiring manager are contacted directly.

What is Interviews?

Interview preparation covers behavioral, technical and executive panel formats, including mock interviews and a reusable story bank.

What is Negotiations?

Negotiation support covers compensation benchmarks, counter-offer scripts and multi-offer strategy.

What is Success?

At the Success step, you decide how to proceed, and KOV supports agreed follow-up and next steps.

What is mock interviews?

Mock interviews are part of interview preparation and help you rehearse for different interview formats.

What is the story bank?

A reusable story bank collects your strongest examples so you can answer interview questions consistently.

What is executive panel interviews?

Interview preparation covers executive panel formats alongside behavioral and technical interviews.

What is compensation benchmarks?

Negotiation support includes compensation benchmarks so you understand the market before you respond to an offer.

What is counter-offer scripts?

Negotiation support includes counter-offer scripts to help you respond clearly and professionally.

What is multi-offer strategy?

Multi-offer strategy helps you compare and sequence offers when more than one is on the table.

What is approval preferences?

Applications are coordinated in line with your approval preferences, so you decide how much you review before anything is sent.

What is the resume and LinkedIn rewrite?

The resume and LinkedIn profile are rewritten during onboarding, using accurate information you approve.

What is targeted applications?

Targeted applications are coordinated in line with the agreed strategy rather than applying indiscriminately.

What is recruiter and hiring-manager contact?

For each role applied to, the process includes contacting the recruiter and hiring manager directly.

What is the search strategy?

The search strategy is set during onboarding and shapes target roles, applications and outreach.

What is goals and target roles?

Goals and target roles are defined at the start so every later step has a clear purpose.

What is follow-up after an offer?

After an offer, you decide how to proceed, and KOV supports agreed follow-up and next steps.

What is the six-step process?

KOV's process has six steps: Onboarding, Job Applications, Outreach, Interviews, Negotiations and Success.

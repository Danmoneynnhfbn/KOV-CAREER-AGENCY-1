FOR SENIOR PROFESSIONALS — Career Representation

Career Representation puts structure behind your search.

KOV works on your side of the job search, coordinating research, positioning and agreed outreach while you remain in control of your decisions.

A senior search can involve more than applications. KOV helps define your target, research relevant opportunities and organize the execution around your priorities.

Discuss Career RepresentationExplore related KOV support

CANDIDATE-SIDE REPRESENTATION

A managed search—not a promise of employment.

In Career Representation, the professional engages support to organize parts of their job search. KOV's work can include research, positioning, opportunity review, applications, recruiter and hiring-manager outreach, follow-up, interview preparation and offer support.

The exact activities and approvals are defined in the engagement. Employers retain control of interviews, selection and offers; KOV does not guarantee interviews, placement or timing.

  • Define target roles, employers and markets
  • Align professional materials with verified experience
  • Research relevant opportunities and organizations
  • Coordinate approved applications and outreach

AGREE THE SEARCH

Set a target that respects your constraints.

Before execution, KOV and the professional discuss seniority, function, industry, geography, timing and practical requirements. Search boundaries can include current-employer exclusions, location constraints and communication preferences.

This makes it possible to focus on roles that fit your priorities rather than measuring progress by application volume alone.

  • Agree must-haves and trade-offs
  • Record organization and role exclusions
  • Confirm approval before external communication
  • Establish reporting and review preferences

PRIVATE, HUMAN-LED EXECUTION

Keep the process visible and the decisions yours.

KOV coordinates agreed search activity and communicates progress using the process established for the engagement. You remain involved in decisions about target fit, applications, external messages and opportunities.

Fees, deliverables and expectations are discussed directly by message and agreed in writing before work begins. Client information is shared only according to the agreed communication process.

  • Review research and proposed opportunities
  • Approve applications and outreach
  • Track responses and next actions
  • Evaluate offers with support if included in scope

COMMON QUESTIONS

Before you start a conversation.

Is Career Representation the same as an employer hiring a recruiter?

No. In KOV's candidate practice, the professional engages KOV for agreed job-search support. Employers make their own hiring decisions and are not represented by this candidate-side service.

Does Career Representation guarantee a job?

No. KOV can manage agreed search activities, but cannot control employer decisions, market conditions or hiring timelines.

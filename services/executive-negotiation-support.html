FOR SENIOR PROFESSIONALS · OFFER STRATEGY

Evaluate the whole opportunity before you respond.

An executive offer is more than a number. Scope, authority, risk, timing and terms can all shape whether the move is right for you.

KOV helps senior professionals organize offer questions and negotiation priorities. Support is based on the information you provide and is not legal, tax or investment advice.

Discuss an OfferExplore related KOV support

ASSESS THE ROLE

Understand what you are being asked to take on.

Before deciding how to respond, clarify the role's mandate, reporting line, resources, decision rights, measures of success and expected working arrangements. A title alone may not explain the practical scope.

KOV can help you list what is known, what remains unclear and which questions should be answered before you commit.

  • Role scope and reporting relationships
  • Resources, authority and success measures
  • Location, schedule and start-date details
  • Questions for the employer or recruiter

REVIEW THE TERMS

Consider the full package and its conditions.

Compensation may include fixed pay, variable pay, equity or other benefits, each with different conditions. The details can vary by employer, jurisdiction and contract, so the written terms matter.

KOV can help organize the questions and priorities you want to raise. For legal, tax or financial interpretation, consult a qualified professional in the relevant jurisdiction.

  • Clarify how variable elements are calculated
  • Understand vesting, eligibility and contingencies
  • Review timing, notice and start-date expectations
  • Seek specialist advice on legal or tax terms

PLAN THE CONVERSATION

Negotiate with a clear rationale and a decision boundary.

A considered negotiation is specific, respectful and grounded in the role and your priorities. Prepare the rationale, the request, the alternatives you can accept and the point at which the opportunity no longer fits.

No result can be promised. KOV's role is to help you assess options and prepare communication; the employer decides what it can offer.

  • Rank priorities before making requests
  • Frame a clear, evidence-based proposal
  • Prepare for different employer responses
  • Keep your final decision with you

COMMON QUESTIONS

Before you start a conversation.

Can KOV guarantee a higher offer?

No. The employer makes the final decision. Support can help you prepare and evaluate options, but it cannot guarantee an increase or a particular term.

Does KOV provide legal or tax advice?

No. KOV can help structure questions, but legal, tax and financial advice should come from qualified professionals familiar with the applicable jurisdiction.

Frequently asked questions

What should a Chief Technology Officer establish before approaching a new employer?

Map the employer's expectations against the Chief Technology Officer remit to set technology direction and connect technical choices with product and business needs. KOV can discuss research and positioning within an agreed engagement.

Which documents help explain a Chief Technology Officer career?

Materials may include executive resume, technology leadership profile, portfolio summary, and LinkedIn profile, selected to suit the recipient and the opportunity. Keep dates and outcomes factual, attribute shared work, and remove confidential information.

How does KOV support a Chief Technology Officer search?

KOV can discuss fit, target strategy, research, positioning, document support, approved outreach, and follow-up for a Chief Technology Officer candidate, according to the service selected. Employers retain control of interviews and hiring decisions; the service does not promise a particular outcome.

How should a Chief Technology Officer describe shared outcomes?

Set out the situation, the decision being made, and your own contribution. A precise attribution is more persuasive than claiming a collective result as an individual achievement.

Can KOV guarantee a Chief Technology Officer interview or appointment?

No. The candidate can decide whether the agreed process and support suit their needs.

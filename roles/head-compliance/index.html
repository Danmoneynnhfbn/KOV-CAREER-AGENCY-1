What should a Head of Compliance establish before approaching a new employer?

Which documents help explain a Head of Compliance career?

Materials may include resume, compliance leadership profile, program summary, and sanitized examples, selected to suit the recipient and the opportunity. Keep dates and outcomes factual, attribute shared work, and remove confidential information.

How does KOV support a Head of Compliance search?

How should a Head of Compliance describe shared outcomes?

Set out the situation, the decision being made, and your own contribution. A precise attribution is more persuasive than claiming a collective result as an individual achievement.

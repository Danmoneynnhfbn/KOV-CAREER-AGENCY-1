What should a Head of Business Development establish before approaching a new employer?

Map the employer's expectations against the Head of Business Development remit to develop relationships and opportunities that support an organization's commercial direction. KOV can discuss research and positioning within an agreed engagement.

Which documents help explain a Head of Business Development career?

Materials may include resume, partnership portfolio, business-development narrative, and profile, selected to suit the recipient and the opportunity. Keep dates and outcomes factual, attribute shared work, and remove confidential information.

How does KOV support a Head of Business Development search?

How should a Head of Business Development describe shared outcomes?

Set out the situation, the decision being made, and your own contribution. A precise attribution is more persuasive than claiming a collective result as an individual achievement.

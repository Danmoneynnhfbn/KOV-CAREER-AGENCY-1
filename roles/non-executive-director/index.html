What should a Non-Executive Director establish before approaching a new employer?

Which documents help explain a Non-Executive Director career?

Materials may include board biography, non-executive profile, skills matrix, and governance resume, selected to suit the recipient and the opportunity. Keep dates and outcomes factual, attribute shared work, and remove confidential information.

How does KOV support a Non-Executive Director search?

How should a Non-Executive Director describe shared outcomes?

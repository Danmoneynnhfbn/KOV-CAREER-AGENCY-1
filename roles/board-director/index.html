What should a Board Director establish before approaching a new employer?

Which documents help explain a Board Director career?

Materials may include board biography, governance resume, skills matrix, and concise profile, selected to suit the recipient and the opportunity. Keep dates and outcomes factual, attribute shared work, and remove confidential information.

How does KOV support a Board Director search?

KOV can discuss fit, target strategy, research, positioning, document support, approved outreach, and follow-up for a Board Director candidate, according to the service selected. Employers retain control of interviews and hiring decisions; the service does not promise a particular outcome.

How should a Board Director describe shared outcomes?

Set out the situation, the decision being made, and your own contribution. A precise attribution is more persuasive than claiming a collective result as an individual achievement.

Can KOV guarantee a Board Director interview or appointment?

No. The candidate can decide whether the agreed process and support suit their needs.

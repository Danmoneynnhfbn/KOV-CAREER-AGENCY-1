FOR CFOs · PRIVATE CAREER REPRESENTATION

A CFO search shaped around the mandate you want next.

Translate your finance leadership record into a focused search across role scope, organization stage, sector and geography.

KOV represents senior professionals in their own job search. CFO support starts by understanding the finance remit you want next and the evidence that demonstrates your fit.

Discuss CFO RepresentationExplore related KOV support

DEFINE YOUR CFO TARGET

Decide which finance mandate matches your experience.

CFO roles vary in ownership, scale and emphasis. A target may involve strategic finance, controls, capital, transformation, investor communication, international operations or a combination shaped by the organization.

KOV helps define what you want to lead, which environments fit your record and which trade-offs—such as company stage, geography or reporting structure—are acceptable.

  • Clarify scope, reporting line and decision authority
  • Choose organization stage and sector priorities
  • Set geography, relocation and timing preferences
  • Separate essential criteria from preferences

MAKE THE FINANCE STORY CLEAR

Show how your decisions changed the business.

Your positioning should explain the scale and context of your remit, the leadership decisions you made and the outcomes you can substantiate. Metrics should be accurate and appropriate to disclose.

KOV can help align your resume, profile, recruiter messages and interview examples so they describe the same leadership value without overstating results.

  • Present verified financial and operating outcomes
  • Explain transformation and governance experience
  • Protect sensitive employer information
  • Prepare concise examples for board and executive audiences

RUN A PRIVATE SEARCH

Research and outreach with clear approval points.

A reverse recruiting engagement may include target-company research, opportunity review, applications, recruiter or hiring-manager outreach, follow-up, interview preparation and offer support. What KOV handles depends on the written agreement.

You remain the decision-maker. KOV cannot guarantee an interview, offer, compensation outcome or placement, and will not disclose your information without the agreed process.

  • Approve targets and external communication
  • Keep search activity aligned with your priorities
  • Review progress and adjust the brief
  • Discuss scope and fees directly before work begins

COMMON QUESTIONS

Before you start a conversation.

Does KOV only represent CFOs?

No. KOV's candidate practice supports senior professionals across roles where the experience and search are a fit.

Will KOV tell my employer I am searching?

Information sharing and outreach approvals are agreed before work begins. Discuss any employer, recruiter or network exclusions directly with KOV.

Common mistakes we correct

A frequent problem is treating engineering modernization as if it were the same as operational leadership. In transportation and logistics, the candidate often needs to show the difference between system design, route optimization, asset strategy, and actual authority over service or network outcomes. That distinction helps employers understand whether the candidate was leading technology change or leading the operating engine behind it.

Another common issue is over-emphasizing technical architecture without clarifying how the candidate supported route performance, customer commitments, asset uptime, or service continuity. KOV works to replace that with a sharper executive story that is specific to transportation and logistics decision-making.

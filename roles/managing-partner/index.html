Frequently asked questions

What should a Managing Partner establish before approaching a new employer?

Map the employer's expectations against the Managing Partner remit to combine leadership responsibilities with the governance and professional context of a partnership. KOV can discuss research and positioning within an agreed engagement.

Which documents help explain a Managing Partner career?

Materials may include partner biography, leadership resume, practice summary, and profile, selected to suit the recipient and the opportunity. Keep dates and outcomes factual, attribute shared work, and remove confidential information.

How does KOV support a Managing Partner search?

KOV can discuss fit, target strategy, research, positioning, document support, approved outreach, and follow-up for a Managing Partner candidate, according to the service selected. Employers retain control of interviews and hiring decisions; the service does not promise a particular outcome.

How should a Managing Partner describe shared outcomes?

Set out the situation, the decision being made, and your own contribution. A precise attribution is more persuasive than claiming a collective result as an individual achievement.

Can KOV guarantee a Managing Partner interview or appointment?

No. The candidate can decide whether the agreed process and support suit their needs.

What should a Chief Strategy Officer establish before approaching a new employer?

Map the employer's expectations against the Chief Strategy Officer remit to support organization-wide choices by framing options, analysis, and strategic execution. KOV can discuss research and positioning within an agreed engagement.

Which documents help explain a Chief Strategy Officer career?

Materials may include executive resume, strategy portfolio, executive biography, and profile, selected to suit the recipient and the opportunity. Keep dates and outcomes factual, attribute shared work, and remove confidential information.

How does KOV support a Chief Strategy Officer search?

How should a Chief Strategy Officer describe shared outcomes?

Set out the situation, the decision being made, and your own contribution. A precise attribution is more persuasive than claiming a collective result as an individual achievement.

How can a Partner choose documents for conversations about Other Services?

How can KOV and a Partner plan contact with Other Services organizations?

Consider the specific organization, the candidate's preferences, and the agreed scope before contact. KOV does not assume a standard Other Services route; research, message, recipient, and disclosure boundaries should be settled with the Partner candidate first.

How can a Partner assess fit with Other Services responsibilities?

No. A familiar title may cover a different mandate, so confirm authority, stakeholders, and expected contribution before judging whether the experience fits.

What can KOV promise a Partner candidate considering Other Services?

KOV does not guarantee introductions, interviews, offers, or employment for a Partner search. Service activity can be agreed with the candidate, but the availability of a contact and any employer response are outside KOV's control.

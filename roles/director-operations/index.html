What should a Director of Operations establish before approaching a new employer?

Which documents help explain a Director of Operations career?

Materials may include resume, operations achievement summary, professional profile, and interview examples, selected to suit the recipient and the opportunity. Keep dates and outcomes factual, attribute shared work, and remove confidential information.

How does KOV support a Director of Operations search?

How should a Director of Operations describe shared outcomes?

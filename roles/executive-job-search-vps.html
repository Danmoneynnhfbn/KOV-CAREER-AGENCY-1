DEFINE THE CONTEXT

Start with the role, market or question—not assumptions.

Vice-president titles vary across sectors and employers. Compare the actual responsibility, team, budget, geography and route to senior leadership when setting targets.

KOV helps vice presidents distinguish roles by remit, decision authority, scale and reporting context—not title alone. The first step is to make the decision criteria clear enough to guide useful research and practical next actions.

  • Specify function and decision scope
  • Compare organization stage and scale
  • Align positioning to verified outcomes

RESEARCH BEFORE ACTION

Use relevant evidence to set priorities.

Specify function and decision scope; Compare organization stage and scale; Align positioning to verified outcomes; Plan applications, outreach and interviews. These considerations help the professional distinguish a genuine fit from a surface-level match.

Titles and broad market labels do not establish fit by themselves. Confirm the remit, requirements and practical boundaries before any application, outreach or shortlist activity.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Before you start a conversation.

What should I share to start?

KOV helps vice presidents distinguish roles by remit, decision authority, scale and reporting context—not title alone. Start with the role or question, relevant markets and practical constraints; share sensitive details only when they are needed.

Does this service guarantee a job, hire or timeline?

No. KOV can carry out only the activities agreed in writing. Employers control hiring decisions, and market conditions and timing remain outside KOV’s control.

How are fees handled?

Fees are discussed directly by message, alongside scope and deliverables, and agreed in writing before work begins.

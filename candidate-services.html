WHY SENIOR PROFESSIONALS USE KOV

Your career search deserves more than another job board.

Senior searches require more than submitting a resume and waiting for a response. The strongest opportunities may come through recruiters, hiring managers, professional networks, target companies, and conversations that never appear on a public job board.

KOV takes responsibility for the work behind the search. Your search is researched. Your positioning is refined. Relevant opportunities are identified. Applications are managed. Recruiters and hiring managers are contacted. Follow-ups are tracked. Interviews are prepared for. Offers are evaluated and negotiated.

You remain the decision-maker. KOV becomes the execution layer behind your search.

THE KOV DIFFERENCE

This is representation, not just coaching.

A coach advises you. A resume writer improves your documents. A recruiter represents an employer.

KOV's reverse recruiting practice is different. KOV works on the candidate's side of the search. The objective is not simply to tell you what to do next. The objective is to execute the work with you and for you.

How KOV Works

WHAT YOU SEE

A search you can actually see.

Your search should not disappear into a black box. Where applicable, KOV uses a shared search process so you can review opportunities, approve roles, track activity, and stay informed as the search progresses.

THE SEARCH PROCESS

From target role to signed offer.

  1. 01 · AUDIT

    We review your background, goals, target roles, market, positioning, and search direction.

  2. 02 · POSITION

    Your resume, LinkedIn profile, messaging, and career story are aligned with the search.

  3. 03 · EXECUTE

    Relevant roles are researched. Applications and outreach begin.

  4. 04 · ENGAGE

    Recruiters and hiring managers are contacted and follow-ups are managed.

  5. 05 · INTERVIEW

    You prepare for interviews with structured strategy, practice, and feedback.

  6. 06 · NEGOTIATE

    When offers arrive, KOV helps you evaluate leverage, compensation, terms, and next steps.

About the founder

Built on credentials.
Driven by strategy.

Adekunle Ayotoba Daniel is the Founder of KOV Career Agency, an independent career representation and talent advisory service dedicated to helping professionals navigate competitive employment markets through strategic positioning, targeted employer engagement, and personalized career support.

His professional foundation spans recruitment, talent acquisition, digital marketing, network marketing, professional communication, and international business engagement. He brings a multidisciplinary perspective to his work, combining market research, strategic thinking, relationship development, and an understanding of how professionals and businesses communicate value in a competitive global environment.

Daniel’s expertise is grounded in an extensive professional credential base in recruitment, sourcing, HR, and career services, including AIRS Professional Recruiter Certification (PRC), AIRS Certified Internet Recruiter (CIR), SHRM Talent Acquisition Specialty Credential, HRCI Global Professional in Human Resources (GPHR), CIPD professional qualifications, REC Certificate in Recruitment Practice (CertRP), AIRS Advanced Certified Internet Recruiter (ACIR), AIRS Certified Technical Recruiter (CTR), AIRS Certified Diversity Recruiter (CDR), Global Certified Diversity Recruiter (gCDR), AIRS Global Certified Sourcing Specialist (gCSS), AIRS Certified Interviewer for Hiring and Recruitment (CIHR), AIRS Recruitment Leadership Certification (RLC), AIRS Certified Recruitment Branding Professional (CRBP), AIRS Certified AI and Sourcing Recruiter (CASR), and NCDA Certified Career Services Provider (CCSP).

At KOV, the work is deliberately strategic: research-led, highly personalized, and designed to replace an unfocused job search with a purposeful representation process that aligns with each client’s goals, market, and opportunity profile.

Why KOV Remains Boutique

The model is intentionally small.

KOV is not built to become a large-volume career service. The boutique model exists so that senior searches can receive direct attention, clear communication, and disciplined execution.

Countries We Serve FAQ, Part 2 (Chad to Ghana)

Questions and answers from KOV Career Agency. Scope is agreed before work begins, fees are confirmed in writing, and no employment, interview or hiring outcome is promised. Contact: daniel@kovcareeragency.org.

Can I work with KOV from Chad while targeting jobs in another country?

Yes in most cases, because KOV works remotely. Support for multi-country opportunities is discussed individually, and you should confirm right-to-work requirements for each country you target.

Can KOV support an executive job search in Chad, including N'Djamena?

KOV can discuss research-led career support for professionals targeting roles in Chad, including N'Djamena. The market pages describe candidate career-search context only, with no local office or guaranteed market access, and no job is guaranteed.

Two fee structures

Global Talent Sourcing (companies): Quoted per engagement based on role level, scope, and timeline - see Global Talent Sourcer for detail.

Reverse Recruiting (candidates): Everything below. No percentage of salary, agreed in writing before anything starts.

01

No % of salary

We never take a cut of your offer or first-year compensation. The fee you agree to is the fee you pay.

02

No fixed tier

A VP search isn't a manager search. We don't pretend a single price tag fits every senior story.

03

No long lock-in

Scope and fee are agreed up front. You can pause or end the engagement with reasonable notice.

How fees are agreed

Separate engagements: Individual career services and private career representation are distinct. The requested work, deliverables and fee are agreed in writing before work begins.

01

Scope first

The fee is based on the specific service and agreed scope, not on registering with KOV or joining a candidate database.

02

Individually agreed

There is no fixed fee for every client. The fee is agreed individually based on your requirements and scope of work.

03

Clear agreement

The fee, deliverables and expectations are agreed in writing before work begins.

What shapes a quotation

The fee follows the work you actually need.

A useful quotation makes the scope visible. It should be clear what KOV will do, what you will receive, what you need to provide and where the work ends.

Complexity and depth

The professional context, number of target directions, research depth and level of preparation required.

Deliverables

The documents, research outputs, profile content, sessions or agreed outreach work to be completed.

Timing and coordination

Practical deadlines, review stages, approvals, communication and any ongoing coordination included.

Revision and support

The review rounds, follow-up, handover or support period included in the written scope.

Before you commit

Clear terms. No implied extras.

What you can expect

  • No fee to make an initial enquiry.
  • Scope and suitability discussed before work begins.
  • Deliverables, fees, responsibilities and payment terms confirmed in writing.
  • Separate services remain separate unless a combined scope is expressly agreed.

What is not assumed

  • Unlimited revisions or indefinite availability.
  • Ongoing representation after a defined engagement ends.
  • Third-party subscriptions, paid advertising, domain or hosting costs.
  • Guaranteed interviews, introductions, offers, employment or search timelines.
  • Any document, application, outreach or coaching work not listed in the agreement.

Common questions

Fees and payment, explained.

Why are fees not displayed as one fixed price?

Representation and individual services involve different deliverables and levels of work. KOV discusses the request and provides a quotation for the actual scope rather than suggesting one fee fits every situation.

Do I pay to register as a candidate?

No. KOV does not charge a candidate registration or recruitment-database subscription fee. A service fee applies only when you choose to proceed with a defined engagement.

Can I buy one service without representation?

Yes. Resume/CV, LinkedIn, career documents, portfolio, personal career website, positioning and coaching are offered as separate Individual Career Services, subject to fit and scope.

Are applications and recruiter outreach included in every package?

No universal package or application volume is assumed. Any search activity, outreach, follow-up or preparation is specified in the representation agreement.

When do I pay?

Payment stages and methods are stated in the written quotation or agreement before work begins. Do not make a payment based on an informal estimate.

Does paying guarantee a job?

No. Fees cover the agreed professional service and deliverables, not a hiring outcome. Employers make their own recruitment decisions.
